In the Court of the District & Sessions Judge,Araria A.B.P. NO. 1952 of 2020 Arising out of Bhargama P.S. case no. 01/2020 Bholu Khan and an other Petitioner. Versus State of Bihar Opposite Party Appearance: For the petitioners: Mr. M.Hussain, Advocate For the prosecution: Mr. L.N.Yadav, P.P. 13.01.2021  This anticipatory bail petition filed on behalf of accused petitioner namely 1. Bholu Khan and 2. Chotu @ Amjad Khan who apprehends arrest in connection with Bhargama P.S. case no. 01/2020 u/s 147, 148, 149, 341, 323, 324, 379, 354, 307, 504, 506 I.P.C. pressed by learned counsel. Out of these two petitioners, one of them namely Bholu Khan has been arrested by the police during pendency of the instant A.B.P. hence the anticipatory bail petition with respect to the petitioner Bholu Khan is dismissed as withdrawn being infructuous and his name has been deleted from the instant A.B.P. Heard both the sides in physical mode of court proceeding. The learned counsel for the accused petitioner submits that the petitioner is innocent who has committed no offence and has been falsely implicated in this case due previous enmity and land dispute and all the offences are bailable except 379,354 and 307 I.P.C. but the allegation for the offences punishable u/s 354 and 379 I.P.C. are general and super addition and offence punishable u/s 307 I.P.C. is not attracted against the petitioner as there is no repetition of blow by the petitioner. There is prior enemical terms running between the parties and petitioner side has also lodged Bhargama P.S. case no. 4/2020 against the informant side, hence the petitioner deserves to be released on anticipatory bail. The learned P.P. opposed the anticipatory bail. Heard the submissions of both the sides and perused the L.C.R. As per allegation of F.I.R. being written report of informant Nasrin Khanam petitioner Chotu @ Amjad Khan carry specific allegation for inflicting fatal assault in fatal manner by stroke of Farsa causing injury on the head of injured Rarwar Khan with intent to kill life. So, considering the nature of allegation and the informant in her restatement in para 6 and witnesses in para 7,8,17 of the case diary have supported the allegation as alleged against the petitioner and investigation is still going on, therefore, I find it is not a fit case to grant anticipatory bail. Accordingly, anticipatory bail prayer of the petitioner Chotu @ Amjad Khan stands rejected. (Dictated) sd/- (P.K.Dixit) Session Judge
