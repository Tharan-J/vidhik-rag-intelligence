IN THE COURT OF THE SESSIONS JUDGE,ARARIA. A.B.P. NO. 1961 of 2020 Arising out of Forbesganj(Simraha) P.S case no. 956/2020 Subham Kr. Gupta and 5 others Petitioner. Versus State of Bihar Opposite Party Appearance For the petitioners: Mr. D.N.Sen,Advocate. For the prosecution: Mr. L.N.Yadav, P.P. 13.01.2021 This anticipatory bail petition filed on behalf of accused petitioners namely 1. Shubham Kumar Gupta 2. Shankar Gupta 3. Geeta Devi 4. Lal Mohan @ Sugam Gupta 5. Lakhan Gupta and 6. Ram Gupta  who apprehend their arrest in connection with Forbesganj(Simraha) P.S. case no. 956/2020 u/s 341, 323, 307,498A /34 I.P.c. & u/s 3/4 of D.P. Act pressed today by learned counsel. Out of the aforesaid six petitioners, two of them namely Shubham Kumar Gupta and 2. Shankar Pd. Gupta have been arrested by the police during pendency of the instant A.B.P. so their bail petition has been dismissed as withdrawn being infructuous and their names have been deleted from the instant A.B.P Heard both the sides in physical mode of court proceeding. The learned counsel for the accused petitioners submits that the accused petitioners are innocent and they have been falsely implicated in this case due to family dispute. They never made any demand nor did they torture or harass the informant in any way. The allegations made by the informant is totally false and fabricated. The learned counsel further submits that the petitioners are inlaws and relatives of the husband of the informant against whom there is no such specific allegation of overt act. Moreover the dispute if any has amicably been settled between the parties, a compromise petition to this effect has also been filed with the bail petition and informant is present in the court to support the factum of compromise, in such circumstances, the petitioners deserve to be released on anticipatory bail., The learned P.P. opposed the anticipatory bail but conceded on the point of compromise. Heard the submission of both the sides and perused the L.C.R. Apparently the informant wife Ruchi Kumari has lodged this case against her petitioner-husband Subham Gupta(now withdrawn due to arrest by the police) and other members and relatives of inlaws family alleging against them all for committing cruelty and harassment for non-fulfilment of dowry demand. Due to amicable settlement of matrimonial dispute between husband and wife as well as with the other members and relative of inlaws family, a joint compromise petition duly signed by the parties and their respective counsel to this effect has been filed with bail petition. As amicable relationship has been restored between the parties, I find it is a fit case to grant anticipatory bail. Accordingly, anticipatory bail prayer of the petitioner no. 3 to 6 is allowed. In the event of their arrest or surrender before the court below within a period of four weeks from receipt of this order, the petitioners namely Geeta Devi, Lal Mohan @ Sugam Gupta, Lakhan Gupta and Ram Gupta shall be released on bail on furnishing their bail bonds of Rs. 5,ooo/- with two sureties of like amount each to the satisfaction of the court below subject to the conditions as laid down u/s 438(2) Cr.P.C.
