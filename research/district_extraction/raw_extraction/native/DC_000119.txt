3.
  In brief, the case as set up by the original plaintiff is that he is
the proprietor of the firm namely M/s Modern Sales and was doing
business of sale of electrical appliances of various brands at its outlet at
SCO No. 1116, Sector 22-B,Chandigarh, he procured an order for
supply of 2492 Air Conditioners of 1.5 tone  each for Sewa Kendras in
Punjab State as per DGS&D rate.  On 18.02.2016, he placed an order to
the defendant No. 1 through Area Sales Manager Sh. Abhishek Sharma.
The defendant No.1 agreed to pay commission @ Rs.2200/- per unit.
Sh. Abhishek Sharma confirmed via emails this offer on 23.02.2016
and 29.03.2016.  The defendant No. 1 was the subsidiary of defendant
No.2. The defendant No.1 supplied Air Conditioners of Voltas make
and installed the same at Sewa Kendras across the Punjab State. The
commission of the plaintiff comes to Rs. 54,82,400/- and defendant No.
1 issued various cheques of different dates.  An amount to the tune of
Rs. 25,50,000/- has been paid in the financial year 2016-2017 after
deducting TDS.  In the year 2017-2018, the plaintiff received an
amount to the tune of Rs. 10,00,000/- and on 28.06.2018, the plaintiff
received an amount in the sum of Rs. 1,14,950/- from the defendants.
The defendants promised to pay the balance amount in the sum of Rs.
15,51,702/-,  however, the defendants did not pay the aforesaid amount.
The plaintiff asked the defendants to pay the aforesaid amount, but in
