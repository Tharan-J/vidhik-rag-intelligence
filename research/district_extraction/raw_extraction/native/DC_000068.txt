J   U   D   G   M   E   N   T
1.
This Criminal Revision has been filed u/s 482 of Bharatiya
Nagarik Sureksha Sanhita, 2023 by the revisionist/petitioner, Dr.
Rajib Kumar Mahotto against the order dated 05.07.2024
passed by the Learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, Bajali, Pathsala
in connection with N.I. Case No. 08/2024 u/s 138 of N.I. Act.
2.
On admission of the revision petition, case record of the
Learned Court below was called for. 
3.
Before going to the merit of the revision, let us see the
leading or relevant facts of the case as reflected in the revision
petition.
The complainant (respondent no. 1 herein) had filed a
complaint case before the Chief Judicial Magistrate, Bajali under
Section 138/142 of N.I. Act stating therein that on 20.02.2023,
the accused (revisionist/petitioner herein) approached him and
asked for borrowing an amount of Rs. 10,00,000/- (Rupees Ten
Lakh) as a temporary loan for the purpose of business, with a
promise to repay the same amount within four months. 
The respondent No. 1 has paid a total of Rs. 10,00,000/-
(Rupees Ten Lakh only) to the revisionist/petitioner on various
dates. It is alleged that, in repayment of the enforceable debt,
the petitioner issued two numbers of Cheque to the respondent

4.
Being highly aggrieved and dissatisfied with the aforesaid
order dated 05.07.2024 passed by the learned Chief Judicial
Magistrate Bajali, Pathsala, the revisionist/petitioner preferred
the instant revision petition before this Court, praying for
setting aside the impugned order dated 05.07.2024 on the
following amongst other grounds :-
i.
That, the learned CJM, Bajali, erroneously passed the
order dated 05.07.2024 despite being no definite ground. 
ii. That, for a prolonged ailment, the petitioner was
physically incapable of appearing before the learned Chief
Judicial Magistrate, Bajali, in NI Case No. 08/2024 until
05.07.2024. Despite this, the learned CJM, Bajali passed a
hasty order based on mere surmise and conjecture,
forming a prima view against the revisionist/petitioner. 
iii. That, the petitioner’s counsel filed an adjournment
petition on 05.07.2024, citing medical grounds and
sought another date for appearance. However, the
learned CJM, Bajali erroneously directed the
revisionist/petitioner to indemnify respondent no. 1,
without granting a hearing, which is illegal, unjust, and
untenable in law, and therefore liable to be set aside. 
In the above premises, the revisionist/petitioner has
prayed for setting aside the impugned order dated 05.07.2024.
POINT FOR DETERMINATION :-
5.
Whether the impugned order dated 05.07.2024 passed by
Learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, Bajali, Pathsala, in N.I. Case
No. 08/2024 suffers from any illegality, incorrectness and
impropriety and as such, requires interference by this Court ?

DISCUSSION, DECISION AND REASONS THEREOF :-
6.
I have heard learned counsel for both the sides. Perused
the case record and the impugned order passed by the learned
Trial court.
7.
Now, on proper scrutiny of the order dated 05.07.2024 of
the Learned Trial Court, it is seen that learned C.J.M., Bajali vide
order dated 05.07.2024 directed the accused to appear on the
next date and make payment, on the oral submission made by
the learned Counsel for the accused that the accused has
through telephonic conversation assured to indemnify the
complainant with the amount of Cheque money. But, on perusal
of the petition no. 2269/2024 submitted by the learned counsel
for the accused Rajib Kumar Mahotto, it is seen that the learned
counsel has sought time for appearance of the accused showing
ground of absence of the accused stating that the accused is
suffering from liver cirrhosis and backbone problem.  Thus, it is
seen that in the instant petition nowhere it is written that the
accused has conceded to make the payment.  Thus, I find that
on the mere oral submission of the learned counsel for the
accused that the accused over telephonic information has
agreed to indemnify the amount of Cheque money to the
complainant and passing order for making payment without
hearing the accused in person, I find that the order passed by
the learned Trial court vide order dated 05.07.2024 suffers from
legal infirmity and hence requires interference by this court. 
ORDER
8.
In the result, the instant revision is allowed on contest by
setting aside the impugned order dated 05.07.2024 passed by
the learned Lower court below with a direction to dispose the
case on merit.
