                           JUDGMENT
This appeal arises from the judgment and order
dated 16.08.2021 passed by the Ld. Judicial Magistrate
First Class-II, Port Blair in G.R. Case No. 606  of  2013
(Trial No. 350 of 2013).   By the said judgment, the Ld.
Trial Court convicted accused T. M. Radhakrishnan for
the offence punishable under section 279/338/304(A) of
Indian Penal Code r.w.s 03 of Prevention of Damages of
Public Property Act, 1984.  
 Against the said judgment, this Criminal Appeal has
been preferred by the Appellant/convict.


--- PAGE 2 ---
2
The brief facts which was alleged in fardbayan is that
on 09.04.2013 at about 5:00 AM, one Inayat Ali, R/o
Dairy Farm came out from his house to the side of the
road to connect the water pipe for collection of some water
and at that time he saw that one bus was coming from
the side of Dairy Farm towards the Dairy Farm Junction
in a very high speed and dashed one motor cycle at that
junction. The said motorcycle was coming from VIP road
side and two persons were sitting on that motorcycles.
After dashing the said motorcycle the bus went towards
the EE, Electricity Office and dashed another person who
was plucking flowers near the electricity office and then
went towards the road side iron fencing and after
breaking the road side fencing the bus stopped on a
chuck hole of pipe lines and also damaged those pipe
lines. Thereafter the driver of the motor cycle who was
badly injured was shifted to G.B Pant Hospital for
treatment with the help of other persons. The pillion rider
of the motorcycle as well the person who was plucking the
flowers, was skittering under the pipe line, were set down
beside the road and by that time PCR arrived. Thereafter,
those victims were also shifted to G.B Pant Hospital.   The
registration number of the offending bus was AN 01 A

7018. The said incident happened due to rash and
negligent driving of the offending bus. Thereafter, said
Inayat Ali came to know that out of three injured persons,
one person died.
Accordingly, a complaint was lodged against the
accused/appellant herein and on the basis of the said

Thereafter, accused appeared before the Ld. Trial

279/338/304(A) of Indian Penal Code  read with section 3
of Prevention of Damages to Public Property Act of the
Indian Penal Code was read over and explained to him in
which he claimed himself as innocent and claim to be
tried.

 During trial of the case, the prosecution examined
22 witnesses. Thereafter, the accused person was
examined under section 313 Cr.P.C. in which he denied
all allegations labeled against him.  
After considering the evidences both oral and
documentary and after hearing arguments from both
sides, the Ld. Lower Court convicted  the accused T.M.
Radhakrishnan for the offence punishable under section
279/338/304(A) of Indian Penal Code read with section 3

and sentenced him to suffer simple imprisonment for one
month and fine of Rs.1000/- for the offence under section
under section 279 of the Indian Penal Code and also to
suffer simple imprisonment of one month and fine of
Rs.1000/- under section 338 of the Indian Penal Code
and also to suffer simple imprisonment of six months and
fine of Rs.5000/- under section 304(A) of the Indian Penal
Code.
Being aggrieved by and dis-satisfied with the
impugned judgment, the appellant/convict preferred the
instant appeal on the grounds stated in the memorandum
of appeal.

POINTS FOR CONSIDERATION THEREFORE ARE:
1.
Whether the impugned judgment passed by  Ld.
Judicial Magistrate First Class-II , Port Blair suffers
from any incorrectness, illegality and impropriety or
not?
2.
Whether the impugned judgment is liable to be set
aside ?
  DECISION WITH REASONS.
Ld. Lawyer of appellant/convict submitted before
this court that offending bus was on slope and fell down
on ditch hence, this accused had no fault accordingly
there was no rash and negligent act from the side of the
accused driver.  Moreover, he was not the regular driver of
said bus and he came on the day of incident to drive the
bus in question. He further submitted that it has also
been stated by PW 21 who was conductor of the said bus
on that relevant day and PW 19 also confirmed the same
stating that bus in question was   in good condition but
had starting problem. He also stated that the accident in
question took place due to often repairing of the bus.  
He further submitted that not a single witness
deposed before this court that the driver of the vehicle


--- PAGE 6 ---
6
was driving the bus in rash and negligent manner and
due to that reason the accident in question took place.  
He also submitted that since the accused driver was
not the regular driver hence, he had no knowledge
regarding the defect in the bus, at the same time he was
also not informed by the owner of the bus or by any
person regarding the defect of the said bus.  Hence,
accident did not take place due to fault of the accused
driver. Hence Ld. Trial Court’s observation that the
accident in question took place due to rash and negligent
driving of the driver of the offending vehicle is not correct.
He also submitted that PW 6, who is a eye witness
also failed to identify the bus in question.  He challenged
the report of M.V.I stating that the observation of said
M.V.I officer is not correct that the accident took place
due to negligence of the offending vehicle. In support of
his contention, he referred eight judgments reported in
2024 Cri.LJ 835, 1971 0 Supreme (SC) 221, 1979 0
Supreme (SC) 320, 2000 (0) Supreme (SC) 1205, 2019 0
Supreme (HP) 384, 1982 0 Supreme (Ori) 48 and 2008 0
Supreme (Mad) 357 and submitted that if prosecution
failed to prove the fact of rash and negligent driving


--- PAGE 7 ---
7
beyond reasonable doubt, it shall be enough to make
possibility of two views and the view which will go in
favour of convict will be taken into consideration. In this
case since rash and negligence has not been proved on
the part of driver accordingly, convict is entitled to be
acquitted from this case.
He also submitted that if the accident will take place
because of mechanical failure and not because of rash
and negligent driving in that case, convict should be
acquitted, which he finds in this case.  He further
submitted that merely from high speed rashness and
negligence cannot be ascertained and there must be
direct nexus between the death of a person and rash and
negligent act of the accused.
He also submitted that there is no evidence that
driver moved bus suddenly before passenger would get
into the bus or the driver moved vehicle even before
getting signal from the  rear side, which is also not in this
case.
He also submitted that in this case, prosecution
failed to prove the case beyond all reasonable shadow of

8
doubt and if a person suddenly comes in front of the bus,
it is very difficult for the driver to save him.
He also submitted that if a person states that the
vehicle was coming with high speed in rash and negligent
manner and the person who saw the accident was
standing within a distance of half Kilometre but not in
close circle then the evidence of that witness cannot be
given much importance. Accordingly, he submitted that
prosecution failed to prove its case beyond all reasonable
shadow of doubt but the Ld. Trial Court forcibly convicted
the convict of this case, which is not a reasoned judgment
and it should be set aside.
On the other hand, Ld. PP submitted before this
court that Ld. Trial Court is perfect in passing his
reasoned judgment as because PW 1 who is the
independent eye witness fully stated about the accident in
question.  He further submitted that PW 6 and PW 8 are
also the eye witnesses of the case rather it can be said
that PW 8 was driving the motorcycle in question, which
met with an accident and he clearly submitting regarding
other facts and circumstances in this case stating that

9
bus was coming from the wrong side and the accident did
not cause due to his negligence. 
He also submitted that the offending vehicle was
coming with high speed.  He further submitted that in
this case total 22 witnesses were examined and Ld. Trial
Court has fully discussed about the evidence in detail
with reason  in his judgment. He further pointed out the
evidence of PW 16 Mechanical Engineer, who examined
the bus and motorcycle and disclosed several defects in
the offending bus, which had no hand brake and had
starting problem. He also stated that the accident in
question took place due to negligence on the part of driver
of the vehicle. Hence, he submitted to affirm the judgment
passed by Ld. Trial Court as it does not suffer from any
irregularities and the judgments cited from the side of
convict are not applicable in this case.
On perusal entire record, submission of Ld. Lawyers
of both sides, I find that PW 1 Inayat Ali, who is one of the
eye witness of the accident, which took place on
09.04.2013 at about 5.00 am, clearly stated that at that
time i.e. 5.00 am, he came out of his house to connect
water pipeline at that time, he saw that one bus was


--- PAGE 10 ---
10
coming from the side of Ganesh Temple with high speed
and dashed a motor cycle at Dairy Farm Junction, which
was coming from VIP road.  He also stated that two
persons were sitting on the motorcycle out of which one
fell down on the road side and another was dragged to the
road side upto iron fencing.  He also stated that one other
person was plucking flower at that time, who was also
dashed.  The bus broke road side fencing and fell down in
the ditch.  He further stated that three persons got
injuries and the accident in question took place due to
fault of driver.  He also stated the number of bus is AN-01
A-4252. He was also knowing the accused driver as
because he had shop in their village, who fled away from
the spot. His evidence was also corroborated by Mukhtior
Mehar, whose house is also situated at Dairy Farm
junction.  He stated that bus fell down into ditch after
dashing the motorcycle. PW 8 Dulal Sarkar, father of
deceased who was driving the motorcycle on that date, is
another eye witness stated that bus was coming from the
wrong side at the time of accident and the accident in
question was not caused due to his negligence.  He
further stated that vehicle was coming with high speed at
the time of accident.  


--- PAGE 11 ---
11
From the evidence of these three witness i.e PW 1,
PW 6 and PW 8, it is very much clear that the offending
vehicle was involved in the accident and at the time of
accident, it was coming with high speed and dashed the
motorcycle.  I also find from the evidence of PW 1 that at
the time of accident it was almost 5.00 am and PW 8 in
his cross examination clearly stated that in the morning
the road was not busy.  If that be so, then it can easily be
construed that the vehicle in question was coming with
high speed.
PW 21 Remon who was the conductor of the said
bus also confirmed that the convict Radha Krishnan was
driving the offending vehicle on that day and he further
stated that he was knowing the repairing of the bus
inspite of same, he took the same. I find from the evidence
of PW 19 i.e. Alagappan, who took the offending vehicle on
lease clearly stated that Radha Krishnan was not the
regular driver but bus was in good condition though it
had starting problem. If that be so, then being a driver
this accused he must have known that the bus had
starting problem when he came and started the bus for
taking the same to ply it but he did not take caution to


--- PAGE 12 ---
12
drive the same or he should had drive it after repairing
the same. 
I also find from the evidence of PW 16 i.e.
Mechanical Engineer that there was no hand break in the
said bus as it was removed. If that be so, then being a
driver, convict must have known that there was much
defect in the bus inspite of that he took the same for its
ply and accordingly, it can be said that it was negligent in
his part at the time of driving the vehicle in question.
I also find that accident took place in early morning
and in the early morning, it can be easily presumed that
there will not so much rush on the road, which has also
been confirmed by the witnesses inspite of that, convict
was driving the vehicle in very speed as it has been stated
by the eye witness.  If that be so, then it can be easily
said that it was the fault of driver to drive the vehicle in
question with high speed knowing fully well that it had
starting problem and had no hand brake, which amounts
to negligence on his part. PW 17 G.Koden Rao, who is
another eye witness also stated that he was plucking
flower at that relevant time when bus dashed him by
breaking iron railing and he got injury.  He also stated


--- PAGE 13 ---
13
that he did not see the driver as because he became
unconscious.  From the evidence of these witnesses, it
can be safely said that the convict was very much
reluctant from its beginning as because the bus in
question had starting problem and without pushing on
the slope, it cannot be started.  If it was so, then he could
have drive the said vehicle with much caution if he took
the vehicle for its ply.  This plea from the side of defence is
not tenable that the driver was not informed about the
condition of the vehicle.  All these things have been fully
discussed in the judgment of Ld. Trial Court and he
discussed the judgment with ingredients. 
 As far as judgments cited from the side of Ld.
Lawyer for the convict is concerned, the witnesses who
saw the accident were in very close proximity of the
accident in question hence, the judgment cited and
reported in 2024 Cri.L.J 835 is not applicable in this
case.  Judgment cited and reported in 1971 0 Supreme
(SC) 621 and 2016 0 Supreme (Raj) 810 are also not
applicable in this case as because it is not the case of
mechanical failure as it appears from the evidence of PW
16 i.e. Mechanical Engineer and in this case, the
motorcycle did not come suddenly in front of the offending


--- PAGE 14 ---
14
vehicle.  As far as judgment reported in 1979 0 Supreme
(SC) 320 and 2000 0 Supreme (SC) 1205 are concerned,
are also not applicable in this case as because in this
case prosecution proved his case beyond all reasonable
shadow of doubt and it is not the case from either side of
the party that driver moved the bus suddenly.  Hence, the
negligence cannot be considered on the part of the driver
in this case.
The judgment cited 2019 0 Supreme (HP) 384, 1982
0 Supreme (Ori) 48 and 2008 0 Supreme (Mad) 357 are
concerned are also not applicable in this case as because
it is not the case of consuming alcohol by the driver or it
is not only the case for driving offending vehicle with high
speed rather it can be said that driver of the vehicle took
the vehicle knowing fully well that the said vehicle had
starting problem and also did not have hand brake inspite
of that he driver the vehicle with high speed.
From the above discussion, I find that the judgment
passed by the Ld. Trial Court is very reasoned judgment
and there is no scope to interfere with the same.  I also do
not find any irregularity in the judgment passed by Ld.
Trial Court as because it is a reasoned judgment.
