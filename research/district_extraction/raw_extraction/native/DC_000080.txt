This anticipatory bail petition filed on behalf of accused petitioner namely Md.
Mujir @ Abdul Kadir Khan who apprehends his arrest in connection with  Narpatganj P.S. case no.
410/2020 u/s 420,406,120B of the  I.P.C. is pressed today.  
Heard both the sides in physical mode of court proceeding.  
The learned counsel for the accused petitioner submits  that the accused petitioner is
innocent who has committed no offence and has been falsely implicated in this case on the basis of
confessional statement of co-accused Md. Mohid with malafide intention. Petitioner has no concern either
with the co-accused of this case or with the alleged offence and no case as alleged is made out against the
petitioner. There is nothing evidence against the petitioner to implicate him in this case, hence this
petitioner deserves to be released on anticipatory bail. 
The learned   P.P.  opposed the anticipatory  bail petition. 
Heard the submission of both the sides and perused the original  L.C.R. Prosecution case
as per F.I.R. being fard bayan of informant Prem Prakash S/O Kumbha Ram, R/O Balia, P.s. Didwana,
Distt. Nagaur, Rajsthan recorded on dated 7.9.2020 near N.H.57 briefly is that on the alleged date and time
i.e. 6.9.2020 at 9 a.m. the informant as trader on continuous persuasion of petitioner Jiwan Ram to purchase
the land in Forbesganj as he also requires to run business motivated the informant for purchasing the land
as he has seen, the informant alongwith co-accused Jiwan Ram and  companion of informant namely
Ramesh Chandra, Bhagwan Ram, Sanjeev Kumar came at Forbesganj and they met to Jiwan Ram and  Hari
Ram  who both told the informant for negotiation with the owner of the land, petitioner Muzir @ Kadir
Khan and after meeting with petitioner Kadir Khan, the informant alongwith  the co-accused  Jiwan Ram
and Hari Ram  were coming to see the plots proposed for purchasing,petitioner Kadir Khan remained at his
house, who has received some calls on his mobile revealing that customers for purchasing the land were
coming and then at around 6 O'clock evening when the informant and other accused persons were sitting at
the house of petitioner Kadir Khan , suddenly on Hulla, Kadir Kha for safety, the informant has handed
over the bag of cash amount of Rs. 25 lacs to Kadir Khan. Suddenly a person riding motorcycle came at the
house of Kadir Khan and asked where they have brought the money to which he told to show a paper first
about the proposed land but he made pretext that first to sow cash amount and then co-accused  Jiwan Ram
so opened the bag of cash amount. In the mean time a female standing near the gate appears to be wife of
the motorcycle rider man and at the same time 10 to 12 persons riding in motorcycle suddenly came there
making cry to flee from spot as the Income Tax persons are coming to raid the house and thus in the mean
time these motorcycle riders snatched the bag containing cash from Jiwan Ram and fled away from the gate
and co-accused Jiwan ram  and Hari Ram also fled away from the  house of petitioner Kadir khan from
back gate. It has been alleged that the petitioner alongwith other co-accused in connivance with each other
on conspiracy have looted away the informant's cash who were in contact with each other through their
mobiles and thus the informant has lodged this case at Naraptganj Area where Place of occurrence lies
against the accused persons including the petitioners who have committed cheating and criminal breach of
trust by taking away the cash amount of informant in pretext of the circumstances developed by the accused
persons and thus besides the cash amount they have also taken away A.T.M. cards, Aadhar Card and money
purse of his friend Amir chand. 
From perusal of the copy of case diary produced showing written up to para 70 by dated
25.9.2020 it transpires that the informant in his restatement in para 2 and his companion and victim Ramesh
Chandra in para 9 and other witnesses in para 10 as well as other companion Bhagwan Ram in para 10 of
the case diary supported the prosecution case. Further in para 16 and onward in case diary that the police
started tracing the accused persons after analysing the C.D.R. of mobile of petitioner  Kadir Khan bearing
no. 9304724711which having found contacted since from dated 6.9.2020  time 18.05.05 hrs with the
mobile no. 7667349793 in different times showing changing of location and last location was Triveniganj,
Supaul and in further investigation police got link with co-accused Md. Mohid and then on raid arrested
Md. Mohid who in his confessional statement as recorded on 9.9.2020 has confessed his involvement
disclosing the name of petitioner Abdul Kadir, his wife Ram Purni, Md. Sajjad, Md. Samshad, Md. Nizam,
Md. Sizam,Md. Samruddin, and 4-5 others having planned to grab the alleged cash amount of informant as
referred in para 26 of the case diary. Further para 38 and onward reveals that police has found link of the
co-accused   named in F.I.R. with the petitioner Abdul Kadir in the alleged crime of forgery, cheating
conspiring for taking away the cash amount of Rs. 25 lacs of the informant and hence many times raided
the abodes of co-accused  and place of their hides and lastly nabbed the co-accused while this accused
petitioner is still evading himself from his arrest against whom investigation is still pending. 
Considering the seriousness of crime, I find it is not a fit case to grant anticpatory bail.
Accordingly, anticipatory bail prayer of the petitioner stands rejected.
