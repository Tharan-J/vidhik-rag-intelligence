ORDERS ON PETITION FILED U/S.13(3) OF BIRTH AND 
DEATH REGISTRATION ACT, R/W. RULE 10(3) OF THE 
REGISTRATION OF BIRTH AND DEATHS RULES.
The petitioners have filed the above petition and sought for 
direction to the respondents to enter the date of death of mother 
of petitioner No.1 and wife of petitioner No.2 by name "Smt. 
Vishranti Ashok Mestri” and issue death certificate accordingly.
2.
Brief facts of the petitioner’s case are as under :
 
 
 
The mother of petitioner No.1 and wife of petitioner No.2 
Late "Smt. Vishranti Ashok Mestri”, died on 23-03-2021 at 
Koganoli village, Taluka. Nipani, leaving behind her, petitioners 
as the only legal heirs.  The date of death of Smt. Vishranti Ashok 
Mestri, was not registered with the respondents and the 
petitioners were under impression that the said date of death of 
Smt. Vishranti Ashok Mestri, was registered in the concerned 
death register through their relatives and as such, till today, the 
petitioners could not register the said date of death of Smt. 
Vishranti Ashok Mestri, in the concerned death register 
maintained by the respondent. When the petitioners approached 
the respondents for issuance of death certificate of Smt. Vishranti 
Ashok Mestri, he issued non-availability certificate. Hence, this 
petition.
3.
The notice of the petition was served on the 
respondents. Though, the notice of the petition was published 
through paper publication neither the respondents nor anybody 
appeared before the court and resisted the application.

Ex.P.1 is the non-availability certificate, Ex.P.2 is the notarized 
copy of Aadhar Card of PW.1, Ex.P3 is the notarized copy of 
Aadhar Card of petitioner No.2, Ex.P4 is the Original Bank of 
India passbook standing in the name of mother of petitioner No.1,
