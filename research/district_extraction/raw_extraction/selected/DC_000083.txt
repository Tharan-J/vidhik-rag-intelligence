This anticipatory bail petition filed on behalf of accused
petitioners namely 1. Raju Hembram 2. Taleshwar Hembram @ Raju Hembram
3. Babuji Hembram 4. Lakshman Hembram @ Laxman Tuddu 5. Tuppan
Hembram @ Chunku Tuddu 6. Baleshwar Kishku and 7. Bajjo Kishku @ Baiju
Kishku  who apprehend their arrest in connection with Raniganj P.S. case no.
513/2020 u/s   147, 148, 149, 341, 323, 327, 385, 386, 447, 504  I.P.C. pressed
today by their learned counsel. 
Heard both the sides in physical mode of court proceeding.
The learned counsel for the accused petitioners  submits that the
accused petitioners are innocent who have committed no offence and  have been
falsely implicated in this case due to previous enmity and land dispute and all the
offences are bailable in nature except 327, 386 I.P.C. which are not directly or
specifically alleged against the petitioners. There is prior enemical terms running
between the parties for the land dispute. As a matter of fact present case is
counter blast of S.C. & S.T. P.S. case no. 31/2020 lodged by the petitioner Babuji
Hembram against the informant and his family, in such circumstances, the
petitioners deserve to be released on anticipatory bail.  
The learned P.P. opposed the anticipatory bail. 
Heard the submissions of both the sides and perused the L.C.R.
Prosecution case as per F.I.R. being written report of informant Santan Jha
briefly is that the informant claims to be purchaser of 10 decs. Land appertaining
to plot no. 338 of khata no. 326,  34 decs. Land appertaining to plot no. 337 of
khata no. 504 in village mauza Parsa Hat by registered sale deed; that on alleged
date 22.10.2020 at 10 a.m. the informant when made objection with the
petitioners and co-accused who all alongwith some unknown persons having
equipped with lathi, farsa, bow arrow illegally started raising hut shet in
informant's land, all the accused persons have refused by giving threatening to
informant for giving rangdari tax of Rs. 10 lacs to retain the possession over the
land in question and thus altercation took place between them and anyhow the
informant escaped from the spot to save his life and to take help of local
administration and police for the alleged highhandedness of accused persons and
accordingly proceeding u/s 107 and 144 Cr.P.c. have been started, hence this case
is filed and registered in bailable offences except 386 I.P.C. 
As per submission of the petitioner, it is evident that no any injury
has been inflicted and it is admitted fact that  there is prior enemical term and
grudge between the parties for land dispute and there is counter allegation of the
petitioners against the informant of this case vide S.C. & S.T. P.s. case no.
31/2020 u/s 147,148,323,354B, 420,120B I.P.C. and u/s 3(1)(r) of S.C. & S.T.
Act  lodged by Babuji Hembram herein petitioner no. 3, I find it is a fit case to
grant anticipatory bail. Accordingly, anticipatory bail petition of the petitioners is
allowed.  In the event of their arrest or surrender before the court below within a
period of four  weeks  from receipt of  the petitioner shall be released on bail on
furnishing their bail bonds of Rs. 10,000/- with two sureties of like amount each
to the satisfaction of the court below subject to the conditions as laid down u/s
438(2) Cr.P.C.
