Smti Yadang Nampe -Vs- Samba Nampe 
 
 
 
Date 
Order

12.12.2025 
This is a domestic violence case being 
registered as Domestic Violence case No. 03/2025 filed 
under section 12 read with sections 2(g), 18, 19, 20, 21, 
22, 23 & 26 of the Protection of Women from Domestic 
Violence Act 2005 for domestic violence of aggrieved 
person Smti Yadang Nampe from her in-laws.    
Heard Ms Yanum Singhi the learned counsel for the 
aggrieved person. 
Along with the domestic violence case, one 
criminal Misc. case being registered as Misc. case No. 
53/2025 has been filed praying for passing ex-parte 
order in favour of the aggrieved person. 
Heard the learned counsel for the aggrieved 
person and after hearing, I am of the considered 
opinion to hear the respondent before passing any 
order for the interest of justice because as on today, 
the point of domestic relationship is yet to be clarified. 
Issue notice to the party for both domestic 
violence case and Misc. case.  
The aggrieved person shall take steps. Dasti 
service is also allowed.  
 
List up the case on 16/01/2025 for appearance of 
the respondent. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
(Damge Niri) 
CJM, Seppa
