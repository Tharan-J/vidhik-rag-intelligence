In the Court of the Sessions Judge,Araria. AB.P.NO.1950/2020 Arising out of Tarabari P.S. case no.108/2020 Mannan and 6 others Petitioner. Versus State of Bihar Opposite Party Appearance: For the petitioners: Mr. M.Hussain,Advocate For the prosecution: Mr. L.N.Yadav,P.P. 07.01.2021 This anticipatory bail petition filed on behalf of accused petitioners namely 1. Mannan 2. Pappu @ Asad 3. Noman 4. Babloo 5. Daraksha 6. Kousar Khatoon and 7. Sabra who apprehend their arrest in connection with Tarabari P.S. case n0. 108/2020 u/s 147,149,341,323,324,354,379,504,506,427 I.P.C pressed today by his learned counsel. Heard both the sides in physical mode of court proceeding. The learned counsel for the accused petitioners submits that the accused petitioners is innocent who has been falsely implicated in this case due to previous enmity and all the offences are bailable except 354 and 379 I.P.C. which are general and super addition. There is prior enemical terms running between the parties and the petitioner Darkhasah has also lodged Tarabari P.S. case no. 104/2020 against the informant and others, hence petitioner deserves to be released on anticipatory bail. The learned P.P. opposed the anticipatory bail petition. Heard the submissions of both the sides and perused the L.C.R.. Prosecution case as per F.I.R. being fard bayan of informant Md. Samim briefly is that all the 7 petitioners alongwith co-accused Asfaque on dated 16.10.2020 at 10 a.m. forming an unlawful assembly having common object came at the house of informant and abused and assaulted to informant and also broke the Tati of courtyard. It has been alleged further that petitioner no. 1 Mannan has inflicted farsa injury on the head of informant and when informant's sister Furiya Khatoon came there, accused persons have also assaulted to Furiya by lathi stroke. There is further allegation that accused persons have committed theft of Rs. 50,000/- cash and Jewelery and ran away seeing the neighbouring persons rushed on the spot. As per submission of the petitioners for having counter allegation against the informant of this case vide Tarabari P.S. case no. 104/2020 lodged by petitioner Darkasha Parween for having prior enemical dispute between both the sides, it transpires that on the alleged date time and place members of both the family came in clash and confrontation and exchanged assault to each other, therefore, allegation of theft and molestation as alleged appears super addition and rest sections of I.P.C. are of bailable in nature. Therefore, in the given facts and circumstances, I find it is a fit case to grant anticipatory bail. Accordingly, anticipatory bail prayer of the petitioners is allowed. In the event of their arrest or surrender before the court below within a period of four weeks from receipt of this order the petitioners shall be released on bail on furnishing their bail bonds of Rs. 10,oo0/- with two sureties of like amount each to the satisfaction of the court below subject to the conditions as laid down u/s 438(2) Cr.p.C.
