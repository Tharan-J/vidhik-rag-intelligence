This case is coming on before me on 21.2.2014 for final hearing
before me in the presence of police for the State and the accused is in
person and after perusing the case record, this court made the following: -
COMMITTAL ORDER

 The Circle Inspector of Police, Chennur has filed charge sheet in Cr.No.
5/1993, PS Chennur for the offence punishable U/s. 147, 148, 302 r/w 149 IPC
and section 25(1)(a), 27 of IA Act against the accused.
2.
The brief facts of the case are that, the accused along with other extremists,
who are belongs Extremists of Chennur Dalam (PWG) deter the deceased Bansilal
from possession of Government land allotted to the deceased in which the
deceased was transplanting Mango sapling under ITDA scheme, the accused
warned the deceased Bansilal, but he did not head to their warnings and continued
transplantation and on 26.3.1993 at 11.00 p.m. The accused along with other
accused came to the house of deceased and surrounded the house and woke the
deceased and took him by the side at a distance of 10 yards and shoot him with
303 rifle and 12 bore guns and went away for defying their warning not to occupy
the land. Thus the accused has committed offence punishable U/sec. 147, 148,
302 r/w 149 IPC and section 25(1)(a), 27 of IA Act. Hence the charge.
