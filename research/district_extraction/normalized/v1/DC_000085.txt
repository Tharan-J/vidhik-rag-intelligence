IN THE COURT OF THE SESSIONS JUDGE,ARARIA. A.B.P. NO. 1949 of 2020 Arising out of complaint case no. 2945-C/2019 Md. Ajaj @ Md. Ejaj Petitioner. Versus State of Bihar Opposite Party Appearance For the petitioners: Mr. Irshad Alam,Advocate. For the prosecution: Mr. L.N.Yadav, P.P. 15.01.2021 This anticipatory bail petition filed on behalf of accused petitioner Md. 2945-C/19 u/s 498A/34 I.P.C. pressed today by his learned counsel. Heard both the sides in physical mode of court proceeding. The learned counsel for the accused submits that the accused petitioner is innocent who never made any demand of dowry nor did he torture or harass the informant in any way. The entire allegations are false and fabricated and this accused petitioner is ready to keep the complainant wife with him with full dignity and honour, hence he deserves to be released on anticipatory bail. The learned P.P. opposed the anticipatory bail petitions. Heard the submission of both the sides and perused the L.C.R. Apparently petitioner is husband of complainant Neha Parween who is present in the court and his wife-complainant is also present in court as for resolving their matrimonial dispute. Neha Parween, wife-complainant has stated in open court for having apprehension of her life therefore she is not ready to go with petitioner-husband to lead conjugal life while the petitioner husband expressed his readiness to keep and maintain his wife with due regard and respect. Maika for the last two years and petitioner-husband has not given her a single money as assistance and maintenance of his wife, so, in the given facts and circumstances and as the petitioner also expressed his willingness to pay some maintenance allowance according to his financial status, the anticipatory bail prayer of the petitioner is allowed. In the event of his arrest or surrender before the court below within a period of four weeks from receipt of this order, the petition shall be released on bail on furnishing his bail bond of Rs. 10,oo0/- with two sureties of like amount each to the satisfaction of the court below subject to the conditions as laid down u/s 438(2) Cr.P.c. with additional conditions that 1. Bailors of the petitioner must be family members or closed relatives. 2. The petitioner at the time of furnishing bail bond shall pay Rs. 2000/- to complainant-wife in court as maintenance amount to complainant and thereafter on the 10th day of each succeeding month the maintenance amount of Rs. 2000/- will be paid to the complainant-wife by the petitioner-husband through court till any maintenance allowance be allowed by the competent court at the instance of complainant-wife. In default to pay the same maintenance amount on two consecutive months, the trial court shall be at liberty to cancel the bail privilege of petitioner after hearing both the sides. (Dictated) sd/- (P.K.Dixit) Sessions Judge
