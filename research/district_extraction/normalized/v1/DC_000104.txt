CNR No:PBJL010004172025 CASE No:446 CRPC-3-2025
STATE OF PUNJAB Vs PUNEET KUMAR SHARMA
Present:
Sh. Anil Gill, Addl.PP for the State
Surety Raj Kumar and Rakesh Sharma in person with
counsel.
In the present case production warrants against accused
Puneet Kumar and Narinder Kumar were issued to Superintendent Jail,
Kapurthala but instead of producing the said accused in the Court, one
copy of letter has been received from the office of CP, Jalandhar whereby
it has been submitted that the aforementioned accused Puneet Kumar and
Narinder Kumar who are gangsters are lodged in Central Jail, Kapurthala
and that they are hard core criminals/gangsters and have connection with
the other gangsters. It is further reported that production of the
aforementioned accused in person in the Court is not safe, accordingly
request is made for getting their presence marked through video
conferencing. Keeping in view the said fact of the case, let the
aforementioned accused persons be produced either in person in Court or
through VC on 02.05.2025. Production warrants of both the accused be
issued to Superintendent Central Jail, Kapurthala.
At this stage, it is submitted by Ld. Counsel for the sureties
that as both the aforesaid accused namely Puneet Kumar and Narinder
Kumar are lodged in Central Jail, Kapurthala and that the present sureties
have traced the whereabouts of the aforesaid accused and thereafter
moved applications in the Court on 07.03.2025 for issuance of their
production warrants. Accordingly, request is made for discharging of the
present sureties. Keeping in view the said facts of the case, this Court is of
the view that although the present sureties have assisted the Court in
tracing the whereabouts of the aforesaid accused persons but nevertheless
there is violation by the present sureties of the surety bonds furnished by
them. Accordingly, separate notices u/s 446 Cr.P.C. served upon both the
aforementioned sureties to which both the aforementioned sureties stated
that I/we has/have traced the whereabouts of the accused Puneet Kumar
and Narinder Kumar and got them produced in the Court. I/We is/are
unable to pay the full surety amount as I/We am/are poor person, so

Requisite amount paid. Remaining amount of the surety bonds are hereby
