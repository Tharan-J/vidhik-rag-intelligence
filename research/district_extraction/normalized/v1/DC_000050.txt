The petitioner has filed the above petition and sought for
direction to the respondents to enter the date of death of

2.
Brief facts of the petitioner's case are as under :

The brother of petitioner late "Krishna Vithoba Ambi", died
on 20.08.1991 at Koganoli village, Taluka. Nipani. The date of
death of Krishna Vithoba Ambi, was not registered with the
respondents and the petitioner was under impression that the
said date of death of Krishna Vithoba Ambi, was registered in the
concerned death register through their relatives and as such, till
today, the petitioner could not register the said date of death of
Krishna Vithoba Ambi, in the concerned death register maintained
by the respondents. When the petitioner approached the
respondents for issuance of death certificate of Krishna Vithoba
Ambi, they issued non-availability certificate hence, this petition.
3.
The notice of the petition was served on the
respondents. Though, the notice of the petition was published
through paper publication. Neither the respondents nor anybody
appeared before the court and resisted the application.
4.
In order to prove the averments of the petition,
petitioner has filed an affidavit in lieu of oral evidence and was
examined as P.W.1 and relied upon 5 documents as per Ex.P.1
to Ex.P.5 and closed his evidence.
5.
Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner. Perused
the materials available on record.

The points that arise for my consideration are:
POINTS
1.
Whether the petitioner is entitled

My findings on the above Points are:
Point No.1: In the Affirmative
Point No.2: As per final order,

 for the following:
REASONS
8.
Point No.1

 :-

In order to substantiate the averments of the petition,
petitioner has filed an affidavit in lieu of chief examination by
reiterating the averments of the petition and examined himself as
P.W.1 and relied upon 5 documents as per Ex.P.1 to Ex.P.5.
Despite service of notice, through paper publication, nobody has
resisted the claim of the petitioner. On the other hand, the
averments of the affidavit of P.W.1, of the petition, remained
unchallenged and non-availability certificate further corroborates
the contention of the petitioner. Ex.P.3 is the RTC extract of
R.S.No.20 situated at Koganoli and name of deceased Krishna
Vithoba Ambi, is appearing. Hence, there is nothing to disbelieve
the version of the petitioner. Accordingly, Point No.1 is answered
in the "Affirmative".
