 :: J U D G M E N T ::
1.
The Circle Inspector of police, Mancherial filed the charge sheet against the
accused No.1 to 10 for the offences under section 147, 148, 427 r/w 149 IPC in
Cr.No. 37/1993 of PS Jaipur.
2.
The brief averments of the charge sheet are as follows:-
Smt. Lambu Anantha Devi is the complainant, PW-1 and Kumri Lambu
Radhika are the husband and daughter respectively of Smt. Lambu Anantha Devi
and they are residents of Kistampur village. A-1 to A-10 (A-10 since died on

16.9.1993 in Cr.No. 83/1993 of PS Jaipur), are the extremists of Chennur dalam
of CPI-ML Group. A-1 is the Dalam Commander A-2 to A-10 are the militants of
Chennur Dalam. A-1 to A-10 bore grudge against PW-1 who is land lord and
Sarpanch of Kistapur village some time prior to the occurrence of the incident of
this case, accused gave threat to PW-1 and due to fear, PW-1 was residing
temporarily at Mancherial. Besides it A-10 threatened the son of PW-1 suspecting
that he was informing their movements to police. On the night of 12.4.1993 Smt.
Anantha Devi along with her daughter Radhfika were sleeping in the house of
one Madhava Reddy in the same village as her husband PW-1 and son were not
present at the house on the intervening night of 12/13.4.1993, A-1 to A-10 formed
themselves into unlawful assembly armed with deadly weapons including guns
went to the house of PW-1 around 0100 hours with common object of causing
wrongful damage to the property of PW-1. A-1 to A-10 blasted the house of PW-1
by using explosives and went away. As a result the house and property all worth
of Rs. 2,05,000/- was damaged. Though the sound of explosion at the house
was heard by Smt. Lambu Anantha Devi and others, none cold dare to go there
immediately, Smt. Lambu Anantha Devi visited the house on the morning of
13.4.1993. on 13.4.1993 on receipt of information about the incident, Md.
Mustafa, S.I of police, Jaipur visited the spot, recorded the statement of Anantha
Devi and referred her statement to police station, Jaipur. PW-3 on 14.4.1993 at
0600 hours registered a case in Cr.No. 37/1993 and took up investigation initially.
In the course of investigation, Md. Mustafa, SI of police, jaipur examined Smt.
Lambu Anantha Devi, PW-1 and Kumari Lambu Radhika and conducted
panchanama of scene of offence before K. Mallaiah and PW-2. The CI of police,
Mancherial namely V Sailu took up further investigation in the case. From the
facts and evidence collected in the investigation it is established that A-1 to A-10
committed the offence punishable under section 147, 148, 427 r/w 149 IPC.
Since A-10, died, A-1 to A-9 are liable for punishment according to law. Hence the
charge.

separated from this case and renumbered as CC 47/2014, as they are

9.
The point for consideration is whether the prosecution has proved the guilt
of A-8 for the offence punishable under section 147, 148, 427 r/w 149 IPC beyond
all reasonable doubt ?
10.
POINT:-
PW-1 deposed that in the year 1990 on one day at about 10.00
a.m. (5) extremists under the leadership of Ramanna, Kanakanna came to his
village, beat him with a stick causing bleeding injuries. Due to attack of said
extremists, he went to Mancherial and living there. On 12.4.1993, the SI of police
Jaipur came to him at Mancherial and informed that the extremists set fire to his
house by using dynamites. At that time, his wife and daughter were sleeping in
his relatives house Madhava Reddy, knowing the incident, his wife and daughter
went to his house and witnessed the incident. Due to the acts of the extremists,
the house hold articles i.e. utencils paddy, TV, carts and records of Gram
Panchayath etc were completely destroyed. His house was also completely
destroyed. The extremists caused the damage of his property upto Rs. 3 lakhs.
On the following day of the incident, police visited the village along with CRP
force of Mancherial and conducted scene of offence panchanama and also
assessed the damage of the property.

11.
PW-2 one of the panch witnesses for the scene of offence, turned hostile
and did not support the case of prosecution. PW-3 is PC 894 who issued First
Information report deposed that on 14.4.1993 while he was in police station, his
the then SI of police forwarded a complaint instructing him to register a case,
upon which he registered a case in Cr.No. 37/1993. Ex.P-1 is said FIR.
Thereafter he handed over the CD file to the SI of police for further investigation.
12.
PW-3 who is daughter of PW1, she deposed that In the year 1993, while
she along with her mother and brother living at Kistapur village. On that day at
about 9.00 p.m she along with her mother went to their neighborer's house and
her brother is not present at that time. Some villagers informed them that
Nuxelites (Annalu) were coming towards their house, by hearing that she and her
mother remained at neighborer's house only. The nuxelites blasted their house by
using explosive and went away. They heard the blasting sound. They not
observed our house due to fear, on the next day morning, she along with her
mother went to their house and observed that their house was damaged which
worth about Rs. 2 lakhs. After that she along with her mother went to Jaipur
police station and lodged complaint against the accused persons. Police
examined her and recorded her statement. In her cross-examination she
admitted that she do not observe, who blasted her house and how her property
was damaged, due to she remained at her neighborer's house at that time.
13.
PW-1 has not deposed anything connecting A-8 in respect of alleged crime.
There is no evidence on record connecting A-8 in respect of alleged crime. The
evidence on record does not show anything as PW-1 has not stated anything
against A-8, So the prosecution has failed to prove the guilt of A-8 for the
offences punishable under section 147, 148, 427 r/w 149 IPC beyond all
reasonable doubt and the accused is entitled under benefit of doubt.
14.
In the result, the accused 8 is found not guilty for the offence punishable
