THE COURT OF ADDITIONAL SESSIONS JUDGE- V, ARARIA
 A.B.P. No. 1947/2021
This anticipatory bail petition filed of behalf of seven accused petitioners
namely, 01. Bablu Raj, 02. Jagdish Mehta, 03. Dev Narayan Mehta, 04. Jagan
Mehta, 05. Ashish Kumar, 06. Abhishek Kumar and 07. Satya Narayan Mehta,
who are apprehending their arrest in connection with Forbesganj P.S. Case
No. 823/2020, U/S 341, 323, 324, 307, 379, 506 r/w 34 of the IPC is pressed
today by Ld. Counsel for the petitioners. Ld. Counsel filed the petition today to
withdraw the name of petitioner No. 7, Satya Narayan Mehta, stated that the
petitioner had died.
The Ld. Counsel for the petitioners submitted that the petitioners are
innocent and they have falsely been implicated in this case due to land
dispute. There is counter case between the parties. There is no any specific
allegation against the accused­petitioners and Sections 307 and 379 of the IPC
which are added to make the case grave and serious in nature. All injuries are
simple in nature, except one injury which is caused by hard blunt substance.
The petitioners have been maliciously and falsely implicated in this case and
the story is totally false and concocted, hence prayed for grant anticipatory
bail for the petitioners.
The Ld. Addl. P.P. has opposed the anticipatory bail petition.
Heard both sides and perused the case record.
The prosecution story in brief is that on 27.09.2020 at around 04:00 PM
The informant saw that the accused Bablu's two cows were ruining his paddy
crop. The informant grabbed both cows and tied them at his door and
informed the Mukhiya. When the informant was showing his ruined crop to
the Mukhiya, meantime all accused persons named in FIR came with arms and
assaulted the informant. Accused Satyanaran Mehta given farsa blow upon
Rupchand Mehta. Accused Jagdish Mehta assault the neck of Rakesh Mehta by
means of sword. Accused Sadanand Mehta assault by means of iron rod upon
head of Vikram Mehta and accused Abhishek Mehta given farsa blow on the
leg of Shekhar Mehta. Accused Deo Narayan Mehta assault the Pankaj Mehta
by means of lathi. Meanwhile, accused Satya Narayan Mehta snatched
motorcycle from the cousin of informant, namely Pankaj Mehta.
From perusal of case record it transpires that there are seven persons
have got injured in the said incident. One of the injured, Vikram Mehta,
suffered with grievous injury, which is located on his head. There is direct and
specific allegations against the accused­petitioners and witnesses have
supported the case of prosecution in their statements and investigation of this
case is still in progress.
Considering the abovementioned facts, I am not inclined to grant the
privilege of anticipatory bail to the petitioners. Accordingly, the anticipatory
bail petition stands rejected.
