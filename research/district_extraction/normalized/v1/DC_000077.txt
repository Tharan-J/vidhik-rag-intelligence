This anticipatory bail petition filed on behalf of accused petitioner
namely 1. Chhotu Ansari who apprehends his arrest in connection with
Sikty(Bardaha) P.S. case no. 175/2020 u/s 341, 323, 328, 307,498A, 506, 504/34
I.P.c. pressed today by learned counsel.
Heard both the sides in physical mode of court proceeding.
The learned counsel for the accused petitioners submits that the
accused petitioner is innocent and he has been falsely implicated in this case due to
family dispute. Petitioner never made any demand nor did he torture or harass the
informant in any way. The allegations made by the informant is totally false and
fabricated. The learned counsel further submits that the dispute if any has amicably
been settled between husband and wife in terms of mutual separation and an affidavit
to this effect has also been filed with the bail petition and informant is present to
support the factum of mutual separation, in such circumstances, the petitioner
deserves to be released on anticipatory bail.
The learned P.P. conceded the submission advanced on behalf of
petitioner.
Heard the submission of both the sides and perused the L.C.R. as well
as affidavit filed by the informant.
Due to amicable settlement of matrimonial dispute between husband
and wife in terms of mutual separation, an affidavit has been filed on behalf of
informant duly identified by her counsel.
As the matrimonial dispute has amicably been settled between
petitioner-husband and informant-wife in terms of mutual separation with each other
and informant-wife by appearing before the court has admitted the factum of
separation and has stated before the court that she has no any grievances from the
petitioner-husband, therefore, I find it is a fit case to grant anticipatory bail.
Accordingly, prayer of the petitioner for anticipatory bail is allowed. In the event of
his arrest or surrender before the court below within a period of four weeks from
receipt of this order, the petitioner shall be released on bail on furnishing his bail
bond of Rs. 10,000/- with two sureties of like amount each to the satisfaction of the
court below subject to the conditions as laid down u/s 438(2) Cr.P.C.
