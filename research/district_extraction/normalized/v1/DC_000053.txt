ORDERS ON PETITION FILED U/S. 13(3) OF THE
BIRTH AND DEATH REGISTRATION ACT R/W. RULE
10(3) OF THE REGISTRATION OF BIRTH AND DEATH
RULES.
The petitioners have filed the above petition and
sought for direction to respondent to enter the date of
death of "Shri. Deepak Dnyandev Sutar" and issue
death certificate accordingly.
2.
Brief facts of the petitioner's case is as

under:
That, late "Shri. Deepak Dnyandev Sutar" is
brother of petitioner No.1 to 4 is died on 01-12-2008. at
Mamdapur (KL) village in Nipani Taluka,. The date of
death of "Shri. Deepak Dnyandev Sutar" was not
registered with respondents. The petitioner under
impression that, the date of death of "Shri. Deepak
Dnyandev Sutar" was registered in the concerned death
register through their other family members or relatives.
The petitioner in order to obtain the varasa certificate
has approached the respondents for issuance of death
certificate of "Shri. Deepak Dnyandev Sutar" and

3
sought issuance death certificate accordingly, respondent
No.1 has issued non-availability certificate on
14.06.2024. Hence, this petition.
3.
The notice of the petition was served on the
respondent. Though, the notice of the petition was
published through paper publication. Neither the
respondent nor anybody appeared before the court and
resisted the application.
4.
In order to prove the averments of the
petition, the petitioner has filed an affidavit in lieu of oral
evidence and was examined as P.W.1 and relied upon
eight documents as per Ex.P.1 to Ex.P.8 and closed his
evidence.
5.
Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner.
Perused the materials available on record.
6.
The points that arise for my consideration are:
1.
Whether the petitioners is
entitled for the relief as
claimed?
2.
What order?
7.
My findings on the above Points are:
Point No.1: In the Affirmative;
Point No.2: As per final order,

REASONS
Point No.1:- In order to substantiate the averments of the
petition, the petitioner has filed an affidavit in lieu of chief
examination by reiterating the averments of the petition and
examined himself as P.W.1 and relied upon five documents as
per Ex.P.1 to Ex.P.5. further petitioners have produced nonavailability certificate of deceased "Shri. Deepak Dnyandev
Sutar" Despite service of notice, through paper publication,
nobody has resisted the claim of the petitioner. On the other
hand, the averments of the affidavit of P.W.1 of the petition,
remained unchallenged and non-availability certificate further
corroborates the contention of the petitioner. Hence, there is
nothing to disbelieve the version of the petitioner. Accordingly,
Point No.1 is answered in the Affirmative.

 The petition is allowed.
