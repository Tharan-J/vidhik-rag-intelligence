JUDGMENT
1.
This is a suit filed by the plaintif against the defendants for
specific performance and agreement of sale Dt:28.03.1988, directing the
defendants to execute a registered sale deed in respect of suit land in favour
of the plaintif, if the defendants failed to execute the sale deed the same
may be done through process of Law.
2.
On 28.03.1988 the defendants No.1 and 2 and the father of
defendant No.3 Late Siddappa entered into an agreement of sale of plaint
schedule land in favour of the plaintif for a valuable consideration of Rs.600/-
and the defendants received the amount and registered the sale deed
whenever the plaintif calls upon them. The plaintif has been ready and
willing to perform and demanded the defendants to execute the sale deed in
favour of the plaintif, but the defendants postponing the execution of sale
deed. After the death of the father of defendant No.3 Late Siddappa,
defendant No.3 has been in possession and enjoyment of the estate of his
father and he has been looking after the afairs of his father as kartha of the
Joint family. Hence the suit.

--- PAGE 2 ---
3.
Defendant No.1 filed written statement and D2 and D3 called
absent and they remained set exparte.
The Counsel for the defendant No.1 contended that, originally the
plaint schedule property and other properties are belongs to one Dodda
Basappa. The said Dodda Basappa having three sons and three daughters by
names are 1) Siddappa, 2) neelakantappa, 3) Veeresh, 4) Lingamma, 5)
Eramma and 6) Nagamma. Further the said property is the jointly and
constructive possession of the estate of Dodda Basappa and the same was
not divided. He further submitted that, one Belagal Mallappa filed a suit for
specific performance against Siddappa and Neelakantappa who are the sons
of Dodda Basappa on the file of this Court in O.S.14/1988 to an extent of
Ac.2.60 cents in Sy.No.422/1A. One Purushothama Reddy is the counsel for
the plaintif in O.S.14/1988 and he also appearing on behalf of the defendant
also. As such, the plaintif counsel behind back to this litigation. The
defendants are illiterate except signature they cannot write and read. Taking
advantage the plaintif manipulated or forged the signature of the defendants
and filed a vexatious suit. Further submitted that, the defendants never
delivered the possession of the plaint schedule properties, due to escalation
of price of land the plaintif filed a frivolous suit and the same was dismissed.
The said Belagal Mallappa filed an appeal in A.S.No.33/1996 on the file of
Senior Civil Judge Court and the same was also dismissed and the second
appeal S.A.P.No.971/2001 on the file of Hon'ble High Court of A.P. is still
pending. Further submitted that as per mandatory provisions of the specific
performance before institutions of suit the plaintif never issued any notice to
the defendants. Hence, they prays this Court to dismiss the suit with
exemplary costs.
4.
On behalf of the plaintif he himself examined as Pw.1 and Ex.A1
is marked.
5.
Basing upon the pleadings the following issues are settled for
trial:
1. Whether plaintif is entitled for specific performance of
 agreement of sale as prayed for ?
2. To what relief ?

--- PAGE 3 ---
6.
Batta not paid. Plaintif called absent. No representation from
16.04.2021. This court adjourned the case from time by giving fair
opportunity to the plaintif to proceed the case i.e., 16.04.2021, 16.09.2021,
04.10.2021, 26.10.2021, 2.11.2021, 8.11.2021, 15.11.2021, 01.12.2021,
13.12.2021, 21.12.2021, 31.12.2021 finally posted today by the condition
order. Inspite of condition order, plaintif called absent. No representation
waited up; to 5.00 PM, it seems that plaintif has no interest to proceed with
the case. Hence, suit is dismissed for default.
Pronounced by me in the open court, this the 04th day of January, 2022.
 Junior Civil Judge,
