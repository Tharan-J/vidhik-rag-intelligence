Court of the Ist Addl. Sessions, Araria.
 A.B.P. No-1953/20.
08.01.201. This anticipatory bail petition filed on behalf of the
petitioner Md. Ashique, who is apprehending his arrest in connection
with Tarabari Case No- 77/20, G.R. No- 2952/20 U/s 147,148,149,341,
323,324,326,307,379,427,504,506/34 IPC is taken up for hearing.
Heard both sides and perused the case record including the
case diary.
As per F.I.R on 11.08.20 at about 2 p.m while the informant
Imran Adil was going to Zamuwa riding on scooti with his nephew
namely Md. Masoom and when reached at Mantu chowk the petitioners
and other co-accused persons named in the FIR surrounded them and
started abusing an on the order of the co-accused Md. Matin the
petitioner gave knife blow upon the nephew of the informant Md.
Masoom due to which he sustained knife blow injury on his ribs and he
fell down and the co-accused Washique assaulted the brother of the
informant namely Abuzar. The co-accused Md. Usman and Md.
Mukhatar assaulted the informant. The injured persons Md. Moosa,
Abuzar and Isha brought to Sadar Hospital, Araria for their treatment.
The learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that the
petitioner is innocent and falsely been implicated in this case due to
local politics and enmity. Allegation is totally false, baseless and absurd.
The offence u.s 307,326 and 379 IPC are omnibus and general in nature
and not attracted against the petitioner as the injured persons sustained
injury in a accident, under such circumstance the petitioner may be
released on anticipatory bail.
The learned Addl. P.P. opposed the prayer for anticipatory
bail.
From perusal of the record it appears that the petitioner is
named in the FIR and there is direct and specific allegation against him
for giving knife blow upon the nephew of the informant Md. Masoom
resulting into his ribs fracture injury which is dangerous weapon on the
vital part f the body as Panjara (in between the stomach, back of the
body) would be caused the death. So, it appears to me that it was
preplanned crime to commit heinous offence with the informant and his
nephew the injured Masoom and others. The informant and the
witnesses have supported the prosecution case vide para-
6,7,8,9,10,27,28,29 of the case diary and still investigation is pending.
Having considered the aforesaid facts and circumstances of
the case, evidence available in the case diary and basically considering
the injury of injured nephew of the informant Md. Masoom and still
investigation is pending, considering all these facts, I am not inclined to
enlarge the petitioner on anticipatory bail and accordingly, this
anticipatory bail petition is hereby rejected.
 (Dictated)

 Sd/-
 Ist Addl. Sessions Judge, Araria.
