This civil appeal being registered as Civil Appeal No.
01/2025 filed under Order 41 read with Rule 1 of the CPC,
1908, read with section 15(proviso) of the Arunachal
Pradesh Civil Court Act 2021 for De Novo trial set aside the
impugned judgment & order dated 12/12/2024 in Cj-
01/2024 passed by Customary court of Shri Radha Tajo
(Head Gaon Burah) inter-village authority in amalgamation
of Gaon Burah's of Chayangtajo area.
Along with the Civil Appeal one interlocutory
application being registered as IA No. 05/2025 has been
filed praying for the condonation of delay of 365 days in
filing appeal under section 5 of the Limitation Act.
Heard the learned counsel Mr Sunday Lingfa and also
perused the case record. Hence, I am of the view that
before admitting the civil appeal No. 01/2025, the court
need to disposed of the application of condonation of delay.
Hence, the application for condonation of delay for
consideration.
Issue notice to the respondent for both Civil Appeal
and IA.
The appellant shall take steps. Dasti service is also
allowed.

List up the case on 16/04/2025 for appearance/written
statement.

(Damge Niri)
