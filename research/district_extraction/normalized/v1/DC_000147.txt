C. R. Case No.- 122 / 2021
 18.04.2024

Out of 04 accuseds, accuseds/ Aboobacker Chamma and Afsa
Guddu are present by filing Hazira and the rest 02 accuseds/
Samsudeen and Rafiq are absent by petition filed U/S 317 of
Cr.P.C. praying to condone their absence inter alia on the plea that
they could not turn up for being held up on the Election Duty.
The aforesaid petition is duly perused, considered and stands
allowed for the ends of Justice considering the aforesaid stated
ground with a direction to get themselves represented through
their Learned Counsel.
Vide an adjournment petition, accommodation is prayed for from
the complainant side inter alia on the plea that due to ill health, the
complainant could not appear before this Court today.
Vehement objection raised from accused side.
Perused above petition. Heard both sides. Considered.
Considering the previous series of Orders passsed by this
Court as on 09.08.2023 and 17.10.2023 respectively alongwith
considering further the fact that several dates have already
lapsed on the aforesaid score for adducing evidence from the
complainant side, prayer for adjournment as sought for
stands allowed for this day, but however, with a specific
direction that in default of the complainant to appear and
adduce evidence on the next scheduled date, no further
opportunity on whatsoever grounds shall be accorded under
any circumstances whatsoever and evidence before charge
shall stand closed.
Fix 11.07.2024 for evidence before charge as per aforesaid
direction and for passing further necessary orders.
Complainant to remain present positively as per aforesaid
direction.
Accuseds as before.
Both parties be intimated accordingly.

Bench Clerk to note.
