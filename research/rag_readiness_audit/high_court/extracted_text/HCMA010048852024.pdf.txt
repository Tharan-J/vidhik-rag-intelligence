This Civil Miscellaneous Petition has 
been filed to condone the delay of 528 days to

1st Respondent in this Second Appeal.

in support of the petition and I am satisfied 
with the reasons stated therein, the delay of 
528 days to bring on record the legal heirs

Nadu Legal Service Authority within a 
period of two weeks.
3. Accordingly, this C.M.P is allowed.
