FINAL ORDER :
 
.
The petitioners are challenging transfer orders effected
during the general transfers for the academic year 2018-2019.
Now the general transfers for the academic year 2019-2020
would be effectuated.  It will not be appropriate, now to decide
these matters claiming transfers on the ground of couple
convenience individually on merits.
2.
The learned counsel for respective petitioners in these
petitions submit that, the petitioners have applied for transfer on
the ground of couple convenience and they have been issued with
the transfer orders beyond the periphery of 30 K.M. thereby
negating very policy as enshrined under the Government
Resolution dated 27.02.2017.  According to petitioners they are
transferred at the distance of 60 to 100 K.M. and the cases for
couple convenience has not been considered.

Parishads submit that all the petitioners have not applied for
transfers on the ground of couple convenience, as such they are
not entitled to claim benefit of couple convenience for transfer.
Those who had applied for transfer on the ground of couple
convenience, their cases are considered and endeavour is made to
transfer them within the periphery of 30 K.M. of the place of
work of their spouse.  In some cases, if the transfers could not be
effectuated within the periphery of 30K.M., they have been
accommodated at the nearest place of work of their spouse.
4.
As now one year has almost lapsed the transfers are
effectuated and the petitioners have joined at transferred places.
There are rival contentions, it would not be possible to evaluate
each and every application given by the petitioners seeking
transfer, so also considering rival contentions.  The petitioners
are contending that they have applied for transfer on the ground
of couple convenience and the respondents are contending that
many of the petitioners have not sought benefit of couple
convenience at the time of transfer.
5.
The Government 
Resolution dated
 27.02.2017
contemplates transfer on the ground of couple convenience on the
terms set out in the G. R.  It further contemplates transfer
within 30 K.M. of the place of work of their spouse.  We may

observe that, the condition of 30 K.M. cannot be construed by
mathematical precision.  For illustration, if the person is
accommodated at distance of 34/35 K.M. of the place of work of
his spouse considering availability of vacancy, it cannot be said
that G. R. dated 27.02.2017 is violated.  However, said condition
has to be observed in its letter and spirit.  Endevour is required
to be made to place the persons eligible for transfer on the
ground of couple convenience within the distance of
approximately 30 K.M from the place of work of their spouse.  Of
course, the same would also depend upon the availability of
vacancies.
6.
Considering the above, we pass following order.
7.
Those petitioners who have applied for transfer on the
ground of couple convenience for the academic year 2018-2019
and those who could not be accommodated within the distance of
30 K.M. of the place of working of the respective spouse, the
respondents shall consider their cases of accommodating them
within the distance of 30 K.M. or nearby distance to the place of
working of their spouse, keeping in mind parameters laid down
in Government Resolution dated 27th February, 2017 and
availability of vacancies.  While taking up this exercise, efforts
should be taken to see that, those persons who are legitimately
working at their allotted place are also not disturbed.
