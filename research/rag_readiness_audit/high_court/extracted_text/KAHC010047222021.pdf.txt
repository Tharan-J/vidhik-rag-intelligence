THESE CRIMINAL PETITIONS ARE COMING ON FOR 
ORDERS, THIS DAY, THE COURT MADE THE FOLLOWING: 
 
 
O R D E R 
 
Criminal Petition No.1027 of 2021 is filed by accused 
No.1 under Section 439 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 
1973, (for short, ‘the Cr.P.C.’) for granting regular bail and 
Criminal Petition No.1035 of 2021 is filed by accused

4 
 
 
Nos.2 to 5 under Section 438 of the Cr.P.C. for granting 
anticipatory bail in  Crime No.7 of 2021 registered by 
Masti Police Station, Kolar, for the offences punishable 
under Sections 417, 420, 498A, 504, 506 and 507 read 
with Section 34 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 (for short, 
‘the IPC’) and Section 3(1)(r)(s) of the Scheduled Castes 
and 
Scheduled 
Tribes 
(Prevention 
of 
Atrocities) 
Amendment Ordinance, 2014 (for short, ‘the SC/ST (POA) 
Act’). 
 
2.  Heard the arguments of the learned counsel for 
the petitioners and the learned High Court Government 
Pleader for the respondent-State. 
 
3. The case of the prosecution is that, on the 
complaint of Smt. Veena V., the Police registered a case.   
It is alleged that the complainant belongs to Scheduled 
Caste.   As the complainant and accused No.1 were loving 
each other, they married on 25-9-2019 in a temple in 
Kailashagiri, Chintamani, and started to reside together.   


--- PAGE 5 ---
                                                                        
5 
 
 
After some time, accused No.1 took her to Mysuru.  They 
stayed up to 18-11-2020.  During that time, accused No.1 
said to have obtained her gold ornaments and sold it.  
Thereafter, accused No.1 brought her to Majestic Bus 
Stand, Bengaluru, that he will take her to his native, but 
left her on the way and went away.   Thereafter, when she 
contacted accused No.1, he did not respond.  She 
contacted the parents of accused No.1 through telephone, 
where they abused her by taking up her caste name.  
Therefore, she lodged a complaint to the Police on           
2-1-2021 and they registered a NCR case, but did not take 
any action.   Again, one more complaint has been filed on 
10-1-2021 and the Police registered the case. During 
investigation, the Police arrested accused No.1 on          
21-1-2021.  However, the Police are making hectic efforts 
to arrest accused No.2 to 5, who are family members of 
accused No.1.  They approached the Sessions Court for 
granting bail, which came to be rejected.    Hence, these 
petitions.   


--- PAGE 6 ---
                                                                        
6 
 
 
 
4.  Learned counsel for the petitioners has 
contended that the petitioners are innocent of the alleged 
offences and they have been falsely implicated.   Accused 
Nos.2 to 5 are not aware of the marriage between the 
complainant and accused No.1.   Even otherwise, accused 
Nos.2 to 5 have not at all involved in commission of any of 
the offences.   The entire allegation goes against accused 
No.1 and he is in custody for more than one month.   The 
only allegation against accused No.2 is that, when the 
complainant telephoned, he abused her by taking up her 
caste name.  Therefore, it will not attract Section 3(1)(r)(s) 
of the SC/ST (POA) Act in order to bring the offence under 
Sections 18 and 18A of the SC/ST (POA) Act. They are 
ready to abide by any of the conditions that may be 
imposed by the Court.   Hence, he prayed for allowing the 
petitions.    
 
5.  Per contra, the learned High Court Government 
Pleader has objected the bail petitions and contended that 


--- PAGE 7 ---
                                                                        
7 
 
 
accused No.1 under the guise of love marriage, married 
the complainant, stayed together for few days in his native 
place and later, shifted to Mysuru.  Over there, he took 
her gold ornaments and sold it. Further, while returning 
back from Mysuru, accused No.1 assured the complainant 
that he will take her to his native, Kolar, but he left her on 
the way near Majestic Bus Stand, Bengaluru, and went 
away.  When the complainant contacted accused No.1, he 
did not respond and when she contacted the parents of 
accused No.1, they have abused her by taking up her 
caste name.   Investigation is under progress and presence 
of accused 
Nos.2 
to 
5 
is 
required 
for 
custodial 
interrogation.  Charge-sheet is yet to be filed.  Hence, she 
prayed for rejecting the petitions. 
 
6.  Learned counsel for the complainant has orally 
objected the bail petitions and contended that, the 
accused persons have committed heinous offence by 
insulting the complainant by taking up her caste name.  


--- PAGE 8 ---
                                                                        
8 
 
 
Further, when the complainant approached the family 
members of accused No.1, they have not let her to enter 
the house.  At the first instance, the Police have not 
registered the case and later, they have registered it.  
Investigation is still pending.   Accused No.1 denied the 
relationship with the complainant in the petition filed 
before the Sessions Judge.   However, he has admitted the 
relationship before this Court.  If bail is granted to 
accused No.1, he is likely to tamper with the prosecution 
witnesses and delay the progress in investigation.  There 
is Bar under Sections 18 and 18A of the SC/ST (POA) Act 
for granting anticipatory bail to accused Nos.2 to 5.  
Hence, he prayed for rejecting the petitions.   
 
7.  Upon considering the arguments and perusal of 
the record, it reveals that accused No.1 and the 
complainant were in love and got married in a temple on 
25-9-2019. They lived in same Village for few days and 
later, shifted to Mysuru. There were residing together till 


--- PAGE 9 ---
                                                                        
9 
 
 
18-11-2020.  Accused No.1 has informed the complainant 
that he is unable to maintain her and wants to go back to 
his native, Kolar, and on the way, he left her in Majestic 
Bus Stand, Bengaluru, and went away.   She tried to 
contact accused No.1, but he did not respond.  Hence, she 
approached accused No.2, father of accused No.1, through 
telephone, where he insulted the complainant by taking 
up her caste name. Looking to the allegations in the 
complaint and documents, it goes to show that accused 
No.1 is solely responsible for commission of the offences 
alleged.   Investigation is under progress.  Except the 
allegation that the complainant contacted accused No.2 
through telephone and he is said to have abused her by 
taking up her caste name, there is no other allegation 
against other accused persons.   Therefore, there is no Bar 
under Sections 18 and 18A of the SC/ST (POA) Act to 
deny anticipatory bail to them, as they have not directly 
involved in committing the offences.   Since accused No.1 
is in judicial custody that itself is not a ground to grant 


--- PAGE 10 ---
                                                                        
10 
 
 
bail to him.   If accused No.1 is released on bail, he is 
likely to tamper with the prosecution witnesses and 
destroying the evidence are not ruled out.   Therefore, I am 
not inclined to grant bail to accused No.1.  Accordingly, 
Criminal Petition No.1027 of 2021 filed by accused No.1 is 
dismissed.
