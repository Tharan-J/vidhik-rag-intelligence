O R D E R 
    
The petitioner with a grievance that his representation, 
seeking permission and police protection for conducting Adal Padal 
programme on 06.06.2025, in connection with the festival of 
Arulmigu Sri Muthumariamman Kovil, situated at Keelathoppu, 
Sellur, Madurai has not been considered by the respondents, has 
approached this Court.
2. The petitioner claims that he is a Committee Member 
of festival committee of  Arulmigu Sri Muthumariamman Kovil, 
situated at Keelathoppu, Sellur, Madurai and according to him, the 
villagers have decided to conduct a festival in the above temple on 
06.06.2025 and therefore, they have submitted a representation to 
the respondents on 18.05.2025 & 24.05.2025. However, the same 
has not been considered by the respondents.  Therefore, the present 
writ petition is filed.

3. Mr.E.Antony Sahaya Prabahar, learned Additional 
Public Prosecutor takes notice for the respondents and submits that 
since there is a possibility of law and order problem, the second 
respondent police has not considered the petitioner's representations. 
4. The learned Counsel for the petitioner submits that the 
festival is being conducted by the entire villagers and there is no 
possibility of any law and order problem. The petitioner also assures 
that the festival will be conducted without any law and order 
problem. In the event, if any law and order problem arises, the 
petitioner undertakes to take responsibility for the same. That apart, 
the learned Counsel for the petitioner also submits that they are 
ready to do some social service to the Local Village Pancahayat, by 
paying a sum of Rs.25,000/- for the purpose of desilting a water 
body in the village.

5. This Court considered the rival submissions made by 
either side.
6. The petitioner's request for conducting the cultural 
programme in connection with the temple festival has not been 
considered by the second respondent on the ground of law and order 
problem. However, the petitioner claims that there is no possibility 
of law and order problem and they will conduct the programme 
without any law and order problem. Moreover, the petitioner is 
prepared to pay a sum of Rs.25,000/- to the Local Village 
Pancahayat for the purpose of desilting a water body in the village. 
7. Considering the submissions made by the learned 
Counsel for the petitioner and also considering the undertaking 
given by the petitioner that he will be responsible for the law and 
order problem if any arises and the petitioner is prepared to do some 
social service by paying a sum of Rs.25,000/- to the Local Village 
Panchayat for the purpose of desilting a water body in the village,
