Property there must be a permanent dedication by a person, any movable or 
immovable property for any purpose recognised by the Muslim law as 
pious, religious or charitable.  The waqf-alal-aulad is dedicated for same 
purpose when the line of succession of the execution fails.  The document 
relating to the waqf Property dated 14.07.1951 when carefully perused, 
though the word “waqf” has been used in the settlement, the intention of the 
settlor was to settle the property to all his legal heirs and the income to be

Though nomenclature of the document appears to be endowment, the
