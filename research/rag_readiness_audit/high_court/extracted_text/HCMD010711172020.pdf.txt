ORDER
(This petition was heard through video conferencing)
Mr.C.Ramesh, learned Special Government Pleader accepts notice
for respondent.  By consent of both sides, this writ petition is
taken up for final disposal at the stage of admission itself.
2.This writ petition has been filed for a Mandamus seeking for
a direction to the respondent to consider the petitioner's
representation dated 21.10.2020 seeking for issuance of three-
wheeler auto rickshaw permit to be used in contract carriage
purposes in favour of the petitioner.
3.It is the case of the petitioner that, he is a three wheeler
auto rickshaw driver and he has the necessary license to drive an
auto rickshaw. It is his case that he had approached the
respondent's office during August 2019 seeking for issuance of new
and fresh three wheeler auto rickshaw permit. According to the

petitioner, the respondent informed him that no permits will be

4.It is the case of the petitioner that on 23.07.2020, this
Court passed an order in W.P.(MD) Nos.6729 to 6734 of 2019 holding
that neither the Central Government nor the State Government has
restricted the grant of new and fresh three wheeler auto rickshaw
permits.
5.According to the petitioner, relying upon the aforesaid
judgment dated 23.07.2020 passed in W.P.(MD) Nos.6729 to 6734 of
2019, the petitioner approached the respondent on 24.08.2020 seeking
for issuance of permit. The petitioner also submitted a
representation on 21.10.2020 to the respondent seeking for fresh
auto rickshaw permit. According to the petitioner, till date, the
said representation has not been considered by the respondent. In
such circumstances, this writ petition has been filed.
6.The relief sought for by the petitioner is an innocuous
relief. No prejudice would be caused to the respondent if the
representation of the petitioner is considered by them on merits and
in accordance with law. 
7.The petitioner in his representation has referred to a
decision of this Court dated 23.07.2020 passed in W.P.(MD) Nos.6729
to 6734 of 2019, wherein this Court has held that neither the
Central Government nor the State Government is restricted to grant
fresh and new three wheeler auto rickshaw permits. The petitioner
had given a representation on 21.10.2020. But till date, the said
representation has not been considered by the respondent.
8.For the foregoing reasons, this Court directs the respondent
to consider the petitioner's representation, dated 21.10.2020
seeking for issuance of a  three-wheeler auto rickshaw permit in his
favour and pass final order on merits  and in accordance with law
and in the light of the decision of this Court, dated 23.07.2020
passed in W.P.(MD) Nos.6729 to 6734 of 2019 within a period of
twelve(12) weeks from the date of receipt of a copy of this order.
9.With the aforesaid directions, this writ petition stands
disposed of. However, there shall be no order as to costs.
