special enactment will have a march over the general 
enactment, as the Criminal Procedure Code is a compilation

special enactment to deal with narcotic and psychotropic 
substances and such other substances.   As stated above, a 
special enactment prevails over the general enactment.  The 
said ratio has been reiterated by the Apex Court in Suresh 
Nanda – Vs – CBI (2008 (3) SCC 674), wherein the Apex 
Court held thus :-
     “8. Thus, the Act is a special Act relating to a
