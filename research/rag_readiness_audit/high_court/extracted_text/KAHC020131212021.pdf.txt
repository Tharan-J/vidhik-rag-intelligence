8. Perused the materials on record. In the light 
of the rival submissions, the point that would arise 
for my consideration is, 
“Whether

My answer to the above point is in the 
‘Affirmative’ for the following reasons:  
R E A S O N S 
 
9. It is the contention of the informant-Sri

driving of the bus by its driver he had fallen in the

12. It is also pertinent to note that the 
Investigating 
Officer 
produced 
the 
postmortem 
report dated 10.02.2020. As per this document, the

of complication of spinal injury. If at all, there was 
semblance of truth in the allegations made in the 
first information and if it has led to the death of the

13. On perusal of these documents, in the light

submissions made by the learned counsel for the 
petitioner and the criminal proceeding initiated
