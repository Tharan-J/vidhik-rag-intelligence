ORDER 
 
 
The counsel for the petitioners have filed memos stating 
that they are not pressing prayer (iv). Submission is taken on 
record. 
 
 
 
2. 
The captioned writ petitions are filed questioning 
the impugned 'Advisory' dated 28.01.2021 issued by the 3rd 
respondent-National Medical Commission and also questioning 
the impugned 'Ordinance Governing Central Assessment 
Program(CAP) for Theory Assessment of MBBS Course' dated 
1.2.2021 issued by the respondent-University.  Consequently, 
a writ of mandamus is also sought against respondents 1 and 
2 to conduct two additional valuations of the failed subjects 
including deviation valuations on there being difference of 
15% marks between all the evaluators strictly in terms of 
Regulation 13(2) of the Medical Council of India Graduate 
Medical Education Regulations, 1997.

academic body, in it's wisdom, has enacted a policy with 
prospective 
effect 
and 
the 
contention 
raised 
by 
the

Regulations, if extended to the petitioners, may open a flood

these set of reasonings, the Co-Ordinate Bench directed the

5. 
The question that survives for consideration is as to 
whether the petitioners are entitled for two additional 
valuations of the failed subjects including deviation valuation.  
Since the said issue is no more res integra in the light of the 
judgment rendered by the Co-Ordinate Bench stated supra, I 
am of the view that the petitioners who are MBBS students 
admitted prior to academic year 2019-2020 are also entitled 
for two additional valuations of failed subjects including 
deviation valuations on there being a difference of 15% marks 
between all the evaluators, strictly in terms of Regulation 
13(2) of the Medical Council of India Graduate Medical 
Education Regulations, 1997.
