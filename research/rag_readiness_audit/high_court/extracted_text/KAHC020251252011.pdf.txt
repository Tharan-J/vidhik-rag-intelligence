16. Insofar as MFA.No.24836/2011 filed by 
the claimant in MVC.No.884/2006 is concerned, 
he was a minor aged about 13 years as on the 
date of accident. The doctor, who assessed the 
disability of the claimant at 25% is not a treated 
doctor. Since he is not a treated doctor, the 
disability is required to be reduced to 1/3rd, 
which would come to 9%. Hence, the claimant 
would 
be 
entitled 
to 
a 
compensation 
at 
Rs.1,00,000/- in terms of the law laid down by 
the Hon’ble Apex Curt in the case of Master 
Mallikarjun Vs Divisional Manager, The National 
Insurance Company Limited & Another reported in 
AIR 2014 Supreme Court 736 wherein the Hon’ble 
Apex Court in paragraph 12 held as follows: 
“Though it is difficult to have an accurate 
assessment of the compensation in the case of 
children suffering disability on account of a motor 
vehicle accident, having regard to the relevant 
factors, precedents and the approach of various 
High Courts, we are of the view that the 
appropriate compensation on all other heads in 
addition to the actual expenditure for treatment, 


--- PAGE 15 ---
 
15
attendant, etc., should be, if the disability is above 
10% and up to 30% to the whole body, Rs.3 
lakhs; up to 60%, Rs.4 lakhs; up to 90%, Rs.5 
lakhs. For permanent disability upto 10%, it 
should be Re.1 lakh, unless there are exceptional 
circumstances to take different yardstick. In the 
instant case, the disability is to the tune of 18%. 
Appellant had a longer period of hospitalization for 
about two months causing also inconvenience and 
loss of earning to the parents.”
