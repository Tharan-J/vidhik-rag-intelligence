JUDGMENT 
 
 
The appeal is filed by the claimant challenging the 
judgment and award dated 18.2.2015 passed by the II 
Additional Senior Civil Judge & MACT, Mandya in MVC 
No.929/2012, seeking enhancement of compensation. 
 
 
 
2. Facts of the case leading rise to filing of this appeal 
briefly stated are as follows : 
 
 
 
On 8.4.2012, at about 9.15 a.m. when the petitioner 
was riding the TVS Scooty bearing No.KA.11-L-1350, slowly 
and cautiously on the left side of the road, from City Market 
towards his house, near Sathyappa’s house, Ashoknagar, 
Mandya, a motor cycle bearing No.KA-11/S-6947, ridden by 
its rider recklessly in a rash and negligent manner, without 
giving any signal came from hind side in a zig zag manner 
and hit the vehicle in which the petitioner was riding.  Due to 
the said impact, the petitioner fell down and sustained severe 
injuries and was immediately taken to General hospital, 
Mandya, wherein he was treated as a inpatient thereby 


--- PAGE 3 ---
 
 
3 
 
incurring huge amount towards medical expenses, hospital 
charges etc.  
 
2.1. The petitioner claimed that he was aged 48 years 
as on the date of accident, hale and healthy prior to the 
accident working as Marketing Executive and also as an LIC 
Agent and was earning Rs.25,000/- per month.  The 
petitioner has further claimed the accident has occurred 
solely due to the rash and negligent riding of the rider of the 
motor cycle and that due to the injuries sustained in the 
accident, he is facing physical discomforts and unable to do 
work as he was doing prior to the accident and hence, filed a 
claim petition against the respondents, owner and insurer of 
the motor cycle claiming compensation of Rs.6,90,000/-.    
 
 
 
2.2. Respondent No.1-owner denied the claim of the 
petitioner but however has stated that as the motor cycle was 
insured with respondent No.2 and that the insurance policy 
was in force as on the date of accident, if at all the any 
compensation is to be paid to the petitioner, the insurer is 
liable to pay the compensation and prayed to dismiss the 
petition against him.

6. Ex.P5 is the wound certificate and Ex.P11 is the X-
ray and from the Doctor’s evidence (PW2) it is proved that 
the claimant has suffered the following injuries : 
1) lacerated wound over the lower lip; 
2) bleeding from the nose; 
3) pain and tenderness at right wrist; 
4) loosing of right central incisor.  
 
 
The wound certificate discloses that there is fracture of 
lowered radius and also subluxation of lower central incisor, 
lateral incisor, canine and premolar of left with central incisor 
of right and injuries No.1 and 2 are simple in nature and 


--- PAGE 6 ---
 
 
6 
 
injuries No.3 and 4 are grievous in nature.  But the Tribunal 
has awarded Rs.25,000/- which is on the lower side.  
Therefore, considering the nature of injuries as stated above, 
I am of the view that a sum of Rs.40,000/- is to be awarded 
to the appellant under the head injuries pain and suffering.  
 
6.1. The appellant has produced medical bills to the 
extent of Rs.2,200/- and the same has been awarded by the 
Tribunal, which needs no modification.  
 
 
 
6.2. The Tribunal has awarded Rs.5,000/- towards 
attendant charges, conveyance and other miscellaneous 
expenses which is on the lower side.  Considering the nature 
of injuries, it is evident that the appellant was not able to 
attend his work for nearly one-and-half months and was 
taking follow-up treatment for which he might have spent for 
transportation, attendant and other related expenses.  Hence, 
a sum of Rs.10,000/- is awarded towards attendant charges, 
conveyance and other miscellaneous expenses.  
 
 
 
6.3. The accident is of the year 2012 and in the 
absence of any evidence to show that the claimant was 
earning Rs.25,000/- p.m., notional income has to be adopted. 


--- PAGE 7 ---
 
 
7 
 
Even though the claimant has produced appointment order 
and identity card to show that he was working as a Marketing 
Executive and getting salary of Rs.25,000/-, he has not 
examined its author or the Authorised Officer of his employer 
to prove the veracity of the said appointment letter and the 
identity card.  Therefore, in the absence of proof of the same, 
notional income of Rs.7,000/- is taken into consideration as 
recognised by the Karnataka State Legal Service Authority for 
the year 2012.   
 
 
6.4. 
Further, 
considering 
the 
nature 
of 
injuries 
sustained by the appellant to various parts of the body, as 
stated above, it might have been difficult for him to attend 
his work atleast for a period of three months and hence, 
requires to be compensated.  Accordingly, taking the notional 
income at Rs.7,000/-, and laid up period of 3 months, the 
appellant is entitled to Rs.21,000/- (7000 x 3) under the head 
loss of income during laid up period. 
 
 
 
6.5. The Tribunal has awarded compensation of 
Rs.20,000/- 
towards 
disability, 
physical 
inconvenience, 
discomfort and loss of future amenities in life.  It is contended

that the Tribunal has not awarded any compensation under 
the head loss of future income on account of the disability 
caused to him and requested to award compensation.   
 
As stated above, the claimant has produced Ex.P9 and 
Ex.P10, which are the Appointment Order and identity card 
only but has not examined his employer or the authorised 
representative of his employer in order to substantiate his 
claim that he was working as a Marketing Executive and 
getting salary of Rs.25,000/- p.m.  Therefore, in the absence 
of any credible evidence in proof of the claim of the appellant, 
those documents cannot be considered.  Furthermore, there 
is also no evidence that the claimant was terminated from the 
services of the Company or that after the accident he was not 
working either as a Marketing Executive or doing any other 
work.  Further regarding his incapacity to do work or his 
removal from the company, there is no evidence.  In the 
circumstances, in the absence of any proof regarding in what 
way the disability is causing functional disability so as to 
cause termination of his service, compensation under the 
head loss of earning capacity due to disability,  cannot be 
awarded.  But at the same time, it cannot be lost sight of the

food, talking etc. Therefore, I deem it just and proper to 
award a sum of Rs.50,000/- under the head ‘disability, 
physical inconvenience, discomfort and loss of future 
amenities in life’.
