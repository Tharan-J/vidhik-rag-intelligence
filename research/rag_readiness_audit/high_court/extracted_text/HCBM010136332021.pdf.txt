P.C. : 
Applicant is aggrieved by order dated 20th March,
2021, passed by learned Additional Sessions Judge, Thane in
Criminal Revision Application No.37 of 2021. The learned Judge
set aside the order dated 9th March, 2021, granting bail to
applicant and directed him to surrender before J.M.F.C., Bhiwandi
on 25th March, 2021.
2
Learned Judicial Magistrate First Class 4th Court
Bhiwandi vide order dated 9th March, 2021, had directed that the
applicant be released on bail in accordance with Section 167(2) of
Cr.P.C.

3
The case of the prosecution is that complainant was
contesting election in Kalher Gram Panchayat. He alongwith his
wife visited Thane and returned home. Thereafter they went
walking at some distance and at that time on 3rd January, 2021,
three rounds were fred at them by unknown persons. They bent
down and avoided injury. Report was lodged with police. During
investigation involvement of applicant and other accused was
revealed. 
4
The applicant was arrested on 7th January, 2021 in
connection with C.R.No.I–6 of 2021, registered at Narpoli police
station for the ofences punishable under Section 307 read with
34 of Indian Peal Code, and, Sections 3, 25 and 27 of Arms Act. 
5
Accused nos.1 to 3 were arrested on 6th January,2021.
They were produced for remand on 7th January, 2021. Applicant
was produced before the Court of J.M.F.C., Bhiwandi on 8th
January, 2021. He was remanded to police custody, and,
thereafter, Magisterial custody. 
6
On 9th March, 2021, the applicant along with three
other accused preferred application for bail under Section 167(2)

within stipulated period of 60 days. The computation of period of

calculated by the applicant was as follows:
1)

7
Learned J.M.F.C. Bhiwandi was pleased to grant bail
to the applicant and the co-accused by order dated 9th March,
2021, in consonance with Section 167(2) of Cr.P.C. While allowing
the said application, it was observed that the accused were
arrested on 6th and 7th January, 2021 and produced before the
Court on 7th and 8th January, 2021. The police did not fle charge –
sheet against the accused. Substantial Sections 5 and 7 of the
Arms Act are not mentioned. Prima facie conclusion about the
application of Section 27 read with Section 7 of the Arms Act
cannot be made. The investigating oficer and APP did not specify

the allegation against Section 27 read with 7 of Arms Act. In the
present case, the ofence punishable under Section 307 Part–I is
punishable with imprisonment extended upto 10 years. 60 days
are completed from the date of production of the accused. It was
further observed that after perusal of remand papers and say of
Assistant Superintendent, it appears that investigating oficer has

submitted that charge–sheet is not fled within stipulated time
and prayed that necessary order be passed, which show that APP

Learned J.M.F.C. granted bail to the applicant as well as to co-

Vijay Bhokare. The said order was challenged by the State by
preferring Criminal Revision Application No.37 of 2021, before

application was preferred on 23rd February, 2021.
8
Learned Sessions Judge vide order dated 20th March,
2021, set aside the order granting default bail to the applicant
and confrmed the order granting bail to the co-accused. Learned

Accused no.4 (Applicant)
The period of custody of the applicant was calculated
as 59 days from 8th January, 2021 to 8th March, 2021. The bail
application was fled on 9th March, 2021 i.e. on 60th day  from frst
day of remand of applicant. It was further observed that
application under Section 167(2) of Cr.P.C. cannot be fled unless
60 or 90 days are completed from frst remand.  In the present
case, applicant has fled application on 60th day without waiting
for expiry of 60 days. The right of accused nos.1 to 3 cannot be
interfered.
9
Mr.Manoj Mohite, Senior Advocate, appearing for the
Applicant assailed the order of the Sessions Court on the ground
that the learned Judge has erred in calculating the period of
custody. The impugned order is contrary to law. There was
miscalculation of period of 60 days. Learned J.M.F.C. has rightly
released the applicant on bail after correctly calculating the
period of 60 days as per the settled principle of law. Learned
Magistrate has rightly observed that the applicant was produced
on 8th January, 2021 and till 9th March, 2021, the charge –sheet
was not fled when the application for bail was preferred. Hence,
60 days were completed from the date of production of accused

State of Maharashtra delivered in LD/VC Criminal Bail

10
Learned APP submitted that there is no infrmity in
the order passed by the learned Sessions Judge. The provisions of

11
Undisputedly, the applicant was arrested on 7th
January, 2021 in connection with C.R. No.I–6 of 2021, registered
with Narpoli Police Station. The applicant was produced before
the Sessions Court for remand on 8th January, 2021. From the
order passed by the learned Sessions Judge, it is apparent that
the Court has accepted the fact that the statutory period for fling
charge–sheet against the accused in this case is 60 days. The
learned Judge has also accepted the fact that 60 days period has
to be calculated from date of frst remand. However, the learned
Judge has apparently miscalculated the days of custody, and,


--- PAGE 8 ---

thereby rendered the impugned decision. The beneft of the
provisions has been given to accused nos.1 to 3. Apparently, their
period of frst day of remand was 7th January, 2021. From the
impugned order, it is apparent that while calculating the period
of custody of accused nos.1 to 3 also there is an error in
calculating the period for the month of January 2021 as 24 days
instead of 25 days. While computing the period of custody of the
applicant, the learned Judge in paragraph no.39 of the order has
observed that the total days in custody from frst day of remand
i.e. 8th January, 2021 to 8th March, 2021 is 59 days. In paragraph
51 of the order, it is observed that, the stipulated period of 60
days gets exhausted on 9th March,2021. The right of accused to
be released on bail gets accrued on completion of 60 days.
Application is fled on 9th March, 2021 i.e. 60 days from the frst
remand of the application. Learned judge has miscalculated the
period from 8th January, 2021 to 8th March,2021 as 59 days
instead of 60 days. The application was preferred on 9th march,
2021 which was the 61st day. Undisputedly, till then, the charge–
sheet was not fled. From the date of production of the accused
before the Court for remand i.e. on 8th January, 2021 till 8th
March, 2021, the period of custody for the month of January 2021
would be 24 days, for February 2021 it would be 28 days, for

March 2021, it would be 8 days. Hence, the application for bail
preferred on 61st day from the date of remand was maintainable
in law, and, therefore, learned J.M.F.C. has rightly granted bail to
the applicant. It is not disputed that till fling of application
charge–sheet was not fled. In the light of the above, the order of
the learned Sessions Judge setting aside order granting bail
passed by the learned J.M.F.C. is required to be set aside.
