ORDER
The prayer sought for herein is for a writ of mandamus
forbearing the respondents 2 and 3, their men and people or
subordinates or any other claiming through them from in any
manner making illegal claim of tax for the non-existing
property namely Door No.24, Car Street, Triplicane, Chennai
- 600 005 (Old CMC  No.0608901210000020). 
2. The property at Door No.24 in Car Street, i.e.,
Theradi Street, Triplicane, Chennai - 5 was originally
belonged to the petitioner and his brother. Thereafter, the
partition seems to have been made under which front portion
was allotted to the petitioner's brother family and the
back portion, i.e., rear portion was allotted to the
petitioner. 
3. Accordingly, it was sub divided as 24/1 [front
portion] and 24/2 [rear portion]. Therefore, the rear
portion 24/2 since allotted to the petitioner, he has been
enjoying the same. 
4. In respect of 24/2 property tax was assessed by the
Corporation of Chennai followed by the Chennai Metro Water,
where the new number 47/2 is taken note of, which is
corresponding to the Old No.24/2.
5. In this regard, earlier occasion, the water tax was
assessed by the Metro water against the petitioner only for
the property at 47/2 [24/2] and recently according to the
petitioner, he has received a whataspp communication from
the Metro water stating that, there was a due of Rs.153/-
as tax, charges and surcharges, which shall be paid jointly
by the    brother Rangarajan of the petitioner and the
petitioner, thereby according to the petitioner once again,
they made a joint demand for the whole property, i.e., Old
No.24 unmindful of the sub-division made in this regard,
therefore the present petition has been filed seeking for a
mandamus forbearing the respondents 2 and 3 from insisting
upon any payment of water tax in the joint name of the
petitioner and his bother for the whole property.
6. Mr.S.Balasubramanian, learned counsel appearing for
the petitioner having reiterated the aforesaid would seek
indulgence of this Court. 
7. Heard Mr.M.Vijayakumar, learned standing counsel
appearing for R2 and R3 and Ms.S.Vaitheeswari, learned
standing counsel appearing for R1.
8. The learned standing counsel appearing for R2 and
R3, i.e., the Metro water, on instruction would submit
https://hcservices.ecourts.gov.in/hcservices/

that, there is no such demand has been made by the Metro
water against the petitioner combining the name of the
petitioner's brother, as if that it is a joint property in
the Door No.24, claiming Rs.153/- as the demand of water
tax, charge and surcharge as stated by the petitioner.
Therefore this can be recorded and the writ petition may be
disposed of, by giving liberty to the respondent Metro
water to issue a proper demand in respect of the
petitioner's divided property, i.e., 24/2 presently 47/2
alone  in the manner known to law.
9. Having considered the said submissions made by both
sides, especially the submission made by the learned
standing counsel for the respondent Metro Water and
recording the said submission made by him, this Court is
inclined to dispose of this writ petition with the
following orders :
Since the property, i.e., Door No.24,
Car Street, Triplicane has been sub
divided between the petitioner and the
petitioner's brother it has been given
a new number called 47/2, which is
equivalent to the original old number,
24/2, i.e., the rear portion of the
property which was allotted to the
petitioner.
If at all any water tax or charges is
due payable by the petitioner for his
property alone to the Metro water, it
is open to the Metro water authority to
make a demand in the manner known to
law for recovery of the same. 
8. With these observation and direction, this writ petition
is disposed of. No costs. Consequently, connected
miscellaneous petition is closed.
