ORDER 
 
 Heard the petitioner’s counsel and also HCGP for 
the respondent-State. 
 
2. 
The factual matrix of the case is that on 
11.01.2018 at about 8 to 9 p.m., the complainant went 
to the house of her son-in-law and at the instance of the 
grand daughter, this petitioner along with accused No.2 
quarreling and beating her daughter and when she tried 
to interfere, assaulted her and they were trying to 
strangulate her and noticing the same, she started 
shouting and neighbours came and rescued the 
complainant and injured, but daughter already lost her 
breathe, hence, the complaint is given and police have 
registered the case against this petitioner and other 
accused for the offences punishable under Sections 
498A, 302, 324, 504 read with Section 34 of IPC, 
investigation has been completed and charge sheet is  
filed. 


--- PAGE 3 ---
 
3
3. 
The petitioner, who is the husband of the 
victim in his petition has contended that due to enmity, 
he has been falsely implicated in the case and further, 
petitioner’s counsel contends that other accused has 
already been enlarged on bail by this Court in Crl.P. 
No.101107/2018 and similar allegation is made against 
this petitioner and hence, this petitioner also stands on 
the same footing and he may be enlarged on bail. 
 
4. 
Per contra, learned HCGP in his arguments 
contends that specific case is made out against this 
petitioner that he only strangulated his wife.  Medical 
records discloses that cause of death was asphyxia due 
to strangulation and there are prima facie materials to 
believe the case of the prosecution that this petitioner 
has only committed the murder of his wife by 
strangulating her by means of plastic wire, hence, this 
petitioner is not entitled for bail. 
 


--- PAGE 4 ---
 
4
5. 
Having 
heard 
the 
arguments 
of 
the 
petitioner’s counsel and also HCGP for the respondent-
State and on perusal of the complaint, specific 
allegation is made that the grand daughter came and 
informed about this petitioner and other accused are 
assaulting 
her 
mother 
and 
immediately, 
the 
complainant rushed to the spot and noticed the incident 
of assault and the allegation is that they were trying to 
hang her daughter and medical records also produced 
before the Court which discloses that cause of death 
was asphyxia due to strangulation and death is also 
occurred in the house of the petitioner.  Having taken 
note of the said fact and also gravity of the offences and 
the very contention of the petitioner’s counsel that the 
other accused has been enlarged on bail cannot be a 
ground to extend the same benefit, since the specific 
allegation is made that this petitioner only strangulated 
his wife by means of plastic wire, hence, it is not a fit
