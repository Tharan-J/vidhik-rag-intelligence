This Writ Petition is filed under Article 227 of the 
Constitution of India, praying to  set aside the order dated  
16-01-2017 passed by the learned II Additional Civil Judge and 
JMFC at Kolar in O.S.No.291/2014, at Annexure E application 
under Order in I.A.No.IX under Order the application on 
I.A.No.IX is filed by the petitioners/plaintiffs under Order 39  
Rule 7 r/w. Section 151 of the CPC for prayed in application that, 
be pleased to preserve the suit items No.1 to 8 in the above case 
until disposal of the suit and allow the application, etc. 
 
This Writ Petition coming on for Preliminary Hearing in ‘B’ 
Group, through Physical Hearing/Video Conferencing Hearing, 
this day, the Court made the following: 
 
O R D E R 
 
 
Being aggrieved by the rejection of their application filed 
under Order XXXIX, Rule 7 read with Section 151 of the Code of 
Civil Procedure, 1908 (hereinafter for brevity referred to as “the 
CPC”), the present petitioners, who were the applicants cum

plaintiffs in O.S.No.291/2014, in the Court of the learned  II 
Additional Civil Judge & Judicial Magistrate First Class, at Kolar 
(hereinafter for brevity referred to as “the Trial Court”), have 
filed the present writ petition. 
 
 
2.  In a suit for partition, the present plaintiffs filed an 
interlocutory application – I.A.No.9 under Order XXXIX, Rule 7 
read with Section 151 of the CPC seeking the Court to preserve 
the suit items No.1 to 8, until the disposal of the suit.  The Trial 
Court by its impugned order dated 16-01-2017, dismissed the 
said application.   
 
3.  The arguments of the learned counsel for the 
petitioners (plaintiffs/applicants) is that, since the suit is pending 
and the defendants are attempting to change the nature of the 
suit by attempting to fix the boundaries, the plaintiffs were 
constrained to file the present application, however, the Trial 
Court did not allow the application for no valid reasons. 
 
4.  Learned counsel for the respondents (defendants) 
submits that the intention of the applicants, if were to see that 
the suit schedule property is not either interfered  or 
intermeddled with some activity, then, they had other ways and

other provisions of law under the very same Code of Civil 
Procedure to pursue for an interim relief, but not the application 
under Order XXXIX , Rule 7 of the CPC.  He also submits that the 
mere reason of the alleged attempt of the defendants to fix the 
boundaries cannot be a ground for seeking the interim relief of 
preservation of the suit property by the Court. 
 
5.  The only reason shown by the applicants  in the Trial 
Court (petitioners herein) seeking for the relief under Order 
XXXIX, Rule 7 of the CPC is that, the defendants in the suit are 
alleged to have been trying to fix the boundaries and demarcate 
the suit property. If that were to be the sole reason, which the 
applicants/plaintiffs intended to prevent, then, it was not 
necessary for them to file an application under Order XXXIX, 
Rule 7 of the CPC seeking the Court to preserve the suit schedule 
items.   They had various other opportunities  under the law 
including the other Rules under the same Code of Civil 
Procedure.  They could have sought the direction by the Court 
against the defendants not to carry out any activities of altering 
the boundaries pending disposal of the case, for which the relief 
of preserving the suit items, that too, by the Court itself need 
not have to be sought for.

6.  Thus, though the Trial Court has dismissed the 
application for some other reason, the final finding that the 
application does not deserve to  be allowed, cannot be interfered 
with. 
Accordingly, the writ petition stands dismissed as devoid of 
merits.
