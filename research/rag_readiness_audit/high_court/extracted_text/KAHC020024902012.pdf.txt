JUDGMENT 
 
Challenging impugned judgment and award 
dated 03.09.2011, Member, MACT-VII, Bilagi, in MVC 
no.228/2009, this appeal is filed by claimant. 
2. 
Brief 
facts 
as 
stated 
are 
that 
on 
19.05.2008, claimant was riding on motorcycle 
bearing registration no.KA-28/K-1716 as pillion, 
when a passenger auto rickshaw bearing registration 
no.kA-29/8990, driven by its driver in rash and 
negligent manner, dashed against motorcycle near 
Muchakhandi cross, on Bagalkot Kaladagi road. Due 
to said accident, claimant sustained grievous injuries. 
Despite taking treatment at District hospital Bagalkot 
and Dr.Kerudi hospital, Bagalkot, he did not recover 
fully. Claiming compensation for same, he filed claim 
petition under Section 166 of Motor Vehicles Act, 
1988, against owner and insurer of auto rickshaw. 
3. On service of notice, owner and insurer entered 
appearance and filed objections. Owner admitted

ownership but denied negligence in causing accident. 
Age, occupation and income, loss of earning capacity 
of claimant was denied. It was however contended 
that vehicle was insured with insurer and policy was 
valid as on date of accident and award if any may be 
passed against insurer. In its objections insurer also 
contended that claim petition was excessive and 
exorbitant. 
4. Based on pleading, tribunal framed issues and 
thereafter recorded evidence of claimant as P.W.1 
and Dr.Hanamant Ramanna Katti as P.W.2 and 
Dr.Maheshkumar Umarani as P.W.3. Exs.P.1 to 179 
were marked. On other hand, insurer examined its 
official as R.W.1. 
5. On consideration, tribunal held that accident 
occurred due to rash and negligent driving of auto 
rickshaw by its driver, insurance policy was valid as 
on date of accident and claimant was entitled for 
compensation. However, claimant did not establish

claimant/appellant submitted that tribunal erred in

of driving licence particulars from RTO and produced 
same in this appeal. It was further submitted that 
licence issued is three wheelers cab which as per 
Section 7 of Motor Vehicles Act, is issued only to 
person who has possessed driving licence to driver 
light motor vehicles for at least one year. Cab which 
is transport vehicle. Since accident occurred two

owner/driver was with effect from 22.07.2008 for a 
period of three years authorizing to drive auto 
rickshaw cab. Section 7 of Motor Vehicle Act, which 
provides for issuance of driving licence of transport 
vehicle is on condition that applicant is required to 
hold driving licence to drive light motor vehicles for 
at least one year. Considering fact that accident 
occurred on 19.05.2008 which is about 2 months 
prior to date of issuance of driving licence in respect 
of auto rickshaw cab, if owner-driver would have held 
that driving licence to drive light motor vehicle for 
period of one year prior to issuance of driving 
licence, then driver would have light motor vehicle as 
on date of accident. Said licence would be sufficient 
in view of ratio in Mukund Dewangan’s case 
(supra).  
12. 
Under such circumstances, discharge of 
liability 
of 
insurer 
would 
not 
be 
justified.
