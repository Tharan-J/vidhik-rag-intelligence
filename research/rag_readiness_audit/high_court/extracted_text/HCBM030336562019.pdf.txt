1.
The petitioners in these petitions assail
the Ordinance No.XXII of 2019 dated 23.08.2019
promulgated by the State of Maharashtra for
extending the election of President, Vice President
and Chairman of subject Committees of some Zilla
Parishads and Chairman and Vice Chairman of
Panchayat Samitis on account of the ensuing General
Election of the State Legislative Assembly.
