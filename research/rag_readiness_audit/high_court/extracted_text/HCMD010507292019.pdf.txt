ORDER
The order of rejection rejecting the claim of the writ
petitioner for retrospective promotion to the post of Additional
Superintendent of Police, on par with the his Junior, is under
challenge in the present writ petition.

2.The writ petitioner is a retired Deputy Superintendent of
Police. He was appointed as Sub-Inspector of Police by way of direct
recruitment. He was promoted to the post of Inspector and to the
post of Assistant Commissioner of Police and subsequently, the
petitioner retired from service on attaining the age of
superannuation on 30.06.2018. When the petitioner was working as
Inspector of Police attached to the Traffic Wing of Thilagar Thidal
Police Station, Madurai District, on 01.08.2007, a criminal case was
registered in Crime No.16 of 2007 against him under Section 7 of the
Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 and departmental disciplinary
proceedings were also initiated.
3.However, the petitioner was exonerated, as the criminal
proceedings were quashed. The departmental disciplinary proceedings
were also disposed of.  The petitioner, after retirement, made a
claim stating that he should be promoted as Additional
Superintendent of Police(ADSP) on par with his junior one
Mr.T.V.Muralidharan.
4.The learned counsel appearing for the petitioner reiterated
that even in the cadre of Sub-Inspector of Police, the said
Muralidharan was junior to the writ petitioner. The junior was
promoted to the post of ADSP and therefore, the petitioner should be
granted promotion on par with his junior notionally with all
consequential benefits.
5.The learned Additional Government Pleader appearing on behalf
of the respondents made a submission that no doubt a criminal case
was registered against the petitioner under the Prevention of
Corruption Act, was quashed. However, the petitioner was involved in
yet another departmental disciplinary proceedings, which was ended
with an order of punishment. During the relevant point of time, when
the said junior Mr.Muralidharan was promoted as DSP, the
petitioner's case was not considered on account of currency of
punishment. Therefore, the petitioner was promoted to the post of
DSP subsequently, after the promotion of his junior Mr.Muralidharan.
Thus, the petitioner became junior to the said Muralidharan in the
cadre of DSP. The post of DSP is the feeder category for the
promotion to the post of ADSP. When the said Muralidharan became
senior in the cadre of DSP, the very reference made by the writ
petitioner is untenable and on that ground, the claim of the writ
petitioner for retrospective promotion was rejected by the competent
authorities. Thus, there is no infirmity in the writ petition and
the same is to be rejected. 
6.The facts are not disputed between the parties. As the writ
petitioner was facing departmental disciplinary proceedings,
punishment was imposed by the disciplinary authority was also not
disputed. Further, the junior Mr.Muralidharan in the category of
Sub-Inspector of Police was promoted to the post of DSP, when the
petitioner was suffering currency of punishment. The said junior was

promoted in the panel of year 2013-2014. The petitioner was promoted
in the year 2009 as DSP. However, the petitioner states that the
petitioner was promoted as DSP in the panel of the year 2009-2010.
Therefore, the petitioner became junior to the said Muralidharan in
the cadre of DSP.
7.When the petitioner became junior on account of currency of
punishment in the cadre of DSP, he cannot refer the said
Muralidharan for the purpose of securing retrospective promotion for
the cadre of DSP. The very claim is based on his junior, became
untenable, as his junior became senior in the cadre of DSP, in view
of the fact the petitioner was suffering currency of punishment
during the particular period. Further, the order impugned also
reveals that the petitioner retired from service on 30.06.2018 and
his name was not considered for inclusion in the temporary panel for
the post of ADSP(AR) for they ear 2018-2019. None of the junior to
the writ petitioner in the category of DSP (category-2) has been
included in the temporary panel to the post of ADSP(Armed reserve)
for the year 2017-2018. This being the factum established, the very
comparison made by the writ petitioner along with Mr.Muralidharan,
is untenable and the reasons stated in the impugned order is
inconsonance with the promotion rules in force.
8.The learned counsel for the petitioner made a submission that
the petitioner may be permitted to file a review petition. It is
always left open to the aggrieved person to approach the competent
authority for redressal  of his grievance. 
9.The relief sought for in the present writ petition cannot be
granted. Accordingly, this writ petition stands dismissed. No costs.
