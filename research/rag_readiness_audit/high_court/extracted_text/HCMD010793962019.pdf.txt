            COMMON ORDER
                *************
[Order of the Court was made by K.RAVICHANDRABAABU.,J.] 
In all these Writ Petitions, the Writ Petitioners are aggrieved
against the proceedings issued under Section 7 of the Tamil Nadu
Land Encroachment Act, 1905, dated 14.08.2019.
2.The impugned proceedings was issued by the fourth
respondent/Revenue Inspector, Kodaikanal Firka, Anna Salai,
Kodaikanal.
3.Mr.VR.Shanmuganathan, learned Special Government Pleader
takes notice for the respondents and by consent of both parties,
these Writ Petitions are taken up for final disposal at the
admission stage itself, since the issue  involved  in all these
cases can be considered and decided  without filing  counter.
4.Treating the  Writ Petitioners as encroachers in respect of
the property referred to in each impugned notice, the fourth
respondent issued  notice under Section 7 of the Tamil Nadu Land
Encroachment Act, 1905, wherein and whereby, the Writ Petitioners
were called upon to  vacate and hand-over the vacant possession of
the respective properties, within a period of 15 days. 
5.The grievance of the Petitioners before this Court is that
the very impugned proceedings itself is in the nature  of an order
of eviction, without giving an opportunity of hearing to the
Petitioners. Therefore, it is contended that  the principles of
natural justice is violated. It is also contended that only  the
third respondent is entitled to pass appropriate orders under  the
Tamil Nadu Land Encroachment Act, 1905 and therefore, the notices
issued by the fourth respondent/The Revenue Inspector  is also bad
in law.

6.On the other hand, the learned Special Government Pleader
appearing for the respondents submitted that the notice issued by
the fourth respondent can be treated as a notice issued by the third
respondent and therefore, the Petitioners can give their
reply/explanations to the third respondent, so as to enable him to
consider those objections and pass appropriate orders on merits and
in accordance with law.
7.There is no dispute to the fact that the impugned notice  is
the  one issued under Section 7 of the Tamil Nadu Land Encroachment
Act, 1905. Needless to state that followed by such notice, a final
order is to be passed by the competent authority under Section 6 of
the Tamil Nadu Land Encroachment Act, 1905, after hearing the
objections raised by the Petitioners. In these cases, though the
said notice was issued by the fourth respondent,  we propose to
remit the matter back to the third respondent for considering and
passing  appropriate orders. Accordingly,  all these Writ Petitions
are disposed of with the following directions:
(a) the impugned notice issued by the fourth respondent shall
be treated as a notice issued by the third respondent under Section
7 of the Tamil Nadu Land Encroachment Act, 1905.
(b)the
 
Petitioners
 
are
 
directed
 
to
 
give
 
their
explanations/objections to the said notices within a period of two
weeks from the date of receipt of a copy of this order;
(c)on receipt of  such explanations/objections, the third
respondent shall pass a speaking order on merits and in accordance
with law under Section 6 of the Tamil Nadu Land Encroachment Act,
1905 within a period of four weeks thereafter; and
(d)till an order is passed by the third respondent, as stated
supra, the status-quo, as on today, shall be maintained by the
respective parties.
No costs. Consequently, connected Miscellaneous Petitions are
closed.
