Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provision Act, 1952. The 
present writ petition is against the claim of interest to the 
tune of Rs.2,47,905/- in terms of the demand notice dated 
07.09.2016 for the belated remittances relating to the period 
from January, 2014 to September, 2015.
2. This Court finds that as far as interest component is 
concerned, there is no question of grant of any waiver. 
Moreover, this Court, in the exercise of its Constitutional 
jurisdiction, cannot delve into the factual disputes as to 
whether the interest was rightly levied or not. The grounds as 
raised in the affidavit are premised on certain factual details 
in regard to certain remittances made by the company. Such 
explanation offered by the petitioner company against the demand 
notice cannot be the subject-matter of adjudication in the writ 
jurisdiction.
3. On behalf of the respondent organization, a decision of 
the Hon'ble Supreme Court in the case of Horticulture Experiment 
Station Gonikoppal, Coorg and Regional Provident Fund 
Organisation (C.A.No.2136 of 2012 dated 23.02.2022) was 
circulated, wherein the Hon'ble Supreme Court has held that mens 
rea consideration was no more available. In any event, as far as 
the claim towards the interest is concerned, no waiver could be 
claimed in the scheme of the Provident Fund Act.
4. When this Court was inclined to dismiss the writ 
petition, learned counsel for the petitioner would request this 
Court to allow payment of interest in three installments, 
commencing from 15.03.2022. According to the learned counsel, 
the petitioner may be permitted to remit the first equated 
monthly installment on 15.03.2022, followed by two other equated 
monthly installments on 15.04.2022 and 15.05.2022.
5. Considering the request made by the petitioner and also 
the facts and circumstances of the case stated in the affidavit, 
the writ petition is dismissed, but with the liberty as sought 
for.
6. Accordingly, the petitioner is permitted to make the 
payment in terms of the demand notice in the writ petition in 
three equated monthly installments, and the first of such 
installment shall commence from 15.03.2022, followed by the 
second and third installments on 15.04.2022 and 15.05.2022, 
respectively.
