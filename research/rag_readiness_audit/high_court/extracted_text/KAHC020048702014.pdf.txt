JUDGMENT 
 
The Insurance Company is in appeal challenging 
the award of compensation in a sum of Rs.27,69,968/- 
which has been awarded to the claimants for the death 
of Smt. Shahida Begum. 
 
2. 
The case put forth before the tribunal was as 
follows; 
On 24.05.2011, Smt. Shahida Begum i.e. wife of 
the first claimant along with her two sons and her 
husband returning from Hangal to Hubli in a Maruti Alto 
Car 
bearing 
registration 
No.KA-25/P-9845 
after 
completion of religious function.  It was stated that at 
about 7.30 a.m., when the said car passed through 
Bankapur, the driver of said car colluded with the KSRTC 
bus bearing registration No.KA-25/F-2614 which was 
crossing PB road and since the driver of said car was 
driving the same in a rash and negligent manner the 
accident occurred. As a result of which grievous injuries 
had sustained to the sons and wife of the first claimant.  
It was stated that Smt. Shahida Begum was taken to the 


--- PAGE 4 ---
 
 
 
: 4 : 
hospital but ultimately she passed away on the way to 
the hospital.  It was also stated that other inmates to 
also passed away due to the said accident. It was also 
stated that the deceased was aged about 48  years and 
was working as Assistant Teacher in Anjuman Urdu 
Primary 
School, 
Bhatkal 
and 
drawing 
salary 
of 
Rs.20,540/-. Due to her death the claimants had lost 
love and affection and the first claimant had lost his life 
partner.  They pleaded that the accident occurred solely 
due to the rash and negligent driving of the driver of the 
car and therefore they have to be made liable for the 
compensation. 
3. 
The respondent No.5 i.e. the driver of KSRTC 
bus though served unrepresented and placed exparte. 
The respondent No.1 did not file objections though he 
had entered appearance.  Respondent Nos.2, 3 and 4 
filed common objections denying the averments of the 
petition.  They pleaded that the accident took place 
solely due to the negligence on the part of the driver of 
the bus bearing registration No.KA-25/F-2614 and that 
there was no negligence on the part of the driver of the

car bearing registration No.KA-25/P-9845. It was stated 
that the first claimant being major and permanent 
employee was not dependant on his wife and therefore 
the question of dependant did not arise.  The respondent 
Nos.3 and 4 have also specifically contended that the 
driver of the car in question was driving the same in a 
rash and negligent manner and as a result of which he 
lost control colluded with the KSRTC bus. It was stated 
that there was absolutely no negligence or rashness on 
the part of the driver of the bus and it was also stated 
that charge sheet was also lead against the driver of the 
said car and sought for dismissal of the petition.  Upon 
hearing the pleadings, the tribunal framed four issues 
and additions issue. 
4. 
The claimant No.1 examined himself as PW.1 
and 
got 
exhibited 
17 
documents. 
On 
behalf 
of 
respondent, two witnesses have been examined as RW.1 
and RW.2 and one document was marked by consent as 
Ex.R-1.  The tribunal on consideration of the material 
placed before it, came to the conclusion that the 
claimants had proved that the deceased Shahida Begum

: 6 : 
was died in a road traffic accident on account of the 
alleged rash and negligent driving of the Maruti Car. The 
tribunal also gave a finding that the Insurance Company 
was unable to prove that the driver of the offending 
vehicle did not hold a valid and effective driving license. 
The tribunal on consideration of the evidence adduced 
before it, came to the conclusion that the claimants were 
entitled to the total compensation of Rs.27,49,968/-. 
5. 
Learned counsel for the Insurance Company 
contended that the tribunal had held in coming to the 
conclusion that negligence was solely on the part of the 
Maruti car as that the vehicle was insured, it was his 
contention that the compensation awarded should have 
been apportioned between the vehicle that was insured 
and the KSRTC bus since both had contributed to the 
accident. 
6. 
Per contra, learned counsel for the claimants 
contended that there was no infirmity in the order of the 
tribunal and the finding recorded by it regarding 
negligence cannot be found fault with and also that the

and

7. 
We have considered the submission made by 
the learned counsel for the parties and we find that there 
is no merit in the appeal for the following reasons.  
8. 
As regard to the negligence, the tribunal has 
considered the evidence of RWs.1 and 2 and come to the 
conclusion that they were only ear say evidence and no 
admission had been made by the Insurance Company to 
examine any eyewitness or the driver of the car to prove 
that there was ailment of negligence on the part of the 
KSRTC bus. The tribunal also come to the conclusion that 
respondent Nos.3 and 4 had also not made any attempts 
to examine the driver or the conductor of the bus. 
However, the evidence of RW.1 indicates that the driver 
of the bus as on the date of accident had been 
discharged from the service and conductor is retired 
from the service. The KSRTC was justified in not 
examining them. The tribunal has also considered the 
spot mahazar and spot sketch as per Ex.P9 and 10 and 
on perusal of the spot sketch has recorded the finding 


--- PAGE 8 ---
 
 
 
: 8 : 
that the KSRTC bus was crossing the PB road and almost 
reached border of said road, at that time, the car came 
and hit in the middle of the bus in between front and 
back wheel and since the PB road was straight road, it 
was clear that the accident occurred only because of the 
rashness and negligence on the part of the car driver.  
9. 
In our view, the tribunal having considered 
the entire material before it and on appreciation of the 
same has come to the right conclusion that the accident 
occurred solely due to the negligence of the driver of the 
car. We find no infirmity or perversity in this appeal and 
therefore affirm the same. 
10. 
Adverting 
to 
the 
issue 
of 
quantum 
of 
compensation awarded, though the learned counsel 
contended that award was excessive, we find that having 
regard to the fact that the deceased was aged 48 years 
and was income tax assessee by earning an annual 
income of Rs.2,44,080/-. The tribunal was right in 
awarding a sum of Rs.27,49,968/- and the tribunal has 
not committed any error in adding future prospects or 
any application of the multiplier. In fact, the tribunal has 


--- PAGE 9 ---
 
 
 
: 9 : 
not granted loss of consortium in favour of the husband 
to the extent indicated by the Hon’ble Apex Court in 
Magma General Insurance Co. Limited v. Nanu Ram 
and Others, 2018 ACJ 2782.  We therefore, find on 
over 
all 
consideration 
of 
the 
matter 
with 
the 
compensation awarded by the tribunal is just and proper 
and does not call for any interference and the appeal 
fails. 
Accordingly, the appeal is dismissed.
