 ORDER
The Criminal Original Petition has been filed to quash the
proceedings in Crime No. 617/2021, on the file of the first
respondent police, for an alleged offences under Sections 294(b),
324 and 506(ii) of IPC.
2.The case is still at the stage of investigation.  By passage
of time, the parties have decided to bury their hatchet and
compromise the dispute amicably among themselves.  
3.A Joint Memo of Compromise has been filed before this Court
which have been signed by the petitioners and the second respondent
and also by their respective counsel. 
https://hcservices.ecourts.gov.in/hcservices/

2
4.Under such circumstances, no useful purpose will be served in
keeping the First Information Report pending before the respondent
police.  Even though, the offences involved are not compoundable in
nature.  In the light of the guidelines given by the Hon'ble Supreme
Court reported in 2017 9 SCC 641-(Parbathbhai Aahir @ Parbathbhai
Vs. State of Gujrath), this Court in exercise of its jurisdiction
under Section 482 Cr.P.C. quashes the First Information Report in
Crime No. 617/2021, on the file of the first respondent police. 
5.This Criminal Original Petition stands allowed and as a
sequel, the proceedings in Crime No. 617/2021, on the file of the
first respondent police, is quashed insofar as the petitioners alone
and the terms of joint compromise memo shall form part and parcel of
this order. Further, the petitioners shall pay a sum of Rs.5,000/-
as costs, to the credit of Government of Tamil Nadu, CMPRF in IOB,
Secretariat Branch, Chennai-9 (Account No.11720 10000 00070, IFSC
Code: IOBA0001172) on or before 20.05.2022 and the receipt of
payment shall be produced before the first respondent, failing which
the order shall stand dismissed automatically without any further
reference to this Court.
