Respondent Nos.1 to 6 who are the plaintiffs had 
instituted the suit in O.S.No.62/2008 against the 
appellants herein, who are defendant Nos.1 to 5 and 
respondent No.7, who is defendant No.6 seeking the 
relief of declaration that the plaintiffs and defendants

Parappa died leaving behind two sons namely 
Murigeppa and Mallappa. They also having died

Murigeppa, the first son of the propositus had a wife

of them, one of the sons namely Shivappa was no 
more, leaving behind his wife and children who are  
plaintiff Nos.1 to 5 and his wife as plaintiff No.6. It 
was contended that plaintiff No.6 is entitled to 1/6th 
share in the 1/6th share of Murigeppa.

5. 
The brothers of late Shivappa i.e., defendant Nos.2 to 
5 were looking after the properties jointly. When the 
plaintiff requested the defendants to give their 
legitimate share in the suit properties, the same was 
postponed and later on denied when the plaintiff came 
to know that defendant Nos.2 to 6 and late Murigeppa 
had fraudulently got mutated their names vide ME 
No.994 in the year 1994. The name of the first 
appellant-defendant No.1 was also mutated for certain 
other lands on the ground that the said Murigeppa 
bequeathed the same to defendant No.1 by way of a 
will. The plaintiff contended that the will and ME 
entries were created with the ill-motive to grab their 
share in the suit properties and as such they were not 
binding on them. The suit properties continued to be 
joint properties of the family, all the parties being in 
joint possession of the same, they were entitled to 
their legitimate share in the said properties.

6. 
On summons being issued, defendants were served 
and they entered appearance through their respective 
counsels. However, despite sufficient opportunity 
having been granted, the defendants failed in filing 
their written statements. The plaintiffs, therefore, led 
their evidence and got marked Exs.P1 to P11 and 
closed their side. The plaintiffs' witnesses were also 
not got cross-examined by the defendants.  
7. 
In such circumstances, the trial Court vide its 
judgment dated 15.02.2016 having considered all the 
documents, which have been produced and taking into 
consideration that there was a joint family in existence 
and the father and husband of the plaintiff Nos.1 to 5 
and 6 respectively having share in the same, decreed 
the suit by holding that the plaintiffs are the legal 
heirs of deceased-Shivappa, who had 1/6th share in 
the suit properties and hence, the plaintiffs being 
class-I heirs are entitled to 1/12th share in 1/6th share 
of the said Shivappa.

In this appeal, I.A.No.2/2020 has been filed to place 
some certain additional documents on record namely 
mutation entry, which had been produced by the 
plaintiffs themselves in the trial Court as also the sale 
deed said to be of the property purchased by second

18. 
I have gone through the said documents; the said 
documents would not further the case of the 
appellants in any manner. The first document being 
that of the mutation entry, which had been produced 
by the plaintiffs themselves and has been considered 
by both the trial Court and the first appellate Court 
and as regards the second document, it cannot be 
said that merely because the property has been 
purchased in the name of second defendant-parappa, 
it is his individual property, when there is nothing on 
record to show that the second defendant-Parappa 
had any other source of income apart from the joint

Having considered and gone through both the
