The present writ petition is filed challenging the order 
dated 19.4.2013 passed by the Industrial Tribunal, Hubballi (for 
short the ‘Tribunal’) in I.D.No.433/2009 wherein the oreder 
dated 
30.01.2001 
 
passed

imposing 
punishment as against respondent herein has been set aside 
and it is held that respondent workman is entitled to the 
monetary benefits which are denied consequent upon the order 
of punishment passed by the petitioner herein. 
2. 
Brief facts of the case as revealed from the records are: 
The 
respondent 
was 
working 
as 
driver 
in 
the 
petitioner/Corporation and while he was in service, on 6/9/1995, 
the respondent met with a road traffic accident and in the said 
accident, the pillion rider of a cycle had succumbed to the 
injuries. On the ground that accident had taken place due to 
rash and negligent driving of the respondent/driver, inquiry was 
held against him and order dated 30.1.2001 was passed against 
him 
reducing 
three 
increments 
with 
cumulative 
effect 
permanently. 
Petitioner/Corporation 
recovered 
sum 
of 
Rs.2,000/- from him in 10 installments as measures of penalty 
for the factual accident caused by him.  


--- PAGE 3 ---
 
 
 
3
3. 
Being aggrieved by the said order dated 30.1.2001, the 
petitioner had filed application for reference and pursuant to the 
same, a dispute was raised before the tribunal and the order 
passed by the petitioner herein was set aside. Being aggrieved 
by the same, the petitioner/Corporation has approached this 
Court. 
4. 
The learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the 
accident had taken place solely due to negligence of the 
respondent/driver and the punishment imposed on him cannot 
be said to be harsh and the petitioner/Corporation has suffered 
huge monetary loss, having regard to the fact that they had to 
pay huge amount towards claim made by the representatives of 
the deceased who had succumbed to the injuries in the accident 
in question. He submits that the tribunal without appreciating 
this aspect has erroneously set aside the order. 
5. 
I have carefully considered the argument addressed by the 
learned counsel for the petitioner and also perused the material 
on record. 
6. 
It is not in dispute that the respondent has been serving 
the petitioner since 1982 and till the accident took place on 


--- PAGE 4 ---
 
 
 
4
6/9/1995, the petitioner had not involved in any other road 
traffic accident. The materials on record would go to show that 
immediately after the accident report has been submitted by the 
Divisional Controller of Kollapur to the Divisional Controller of 
Belagavi wherein it has been stated that bus bearing No.KA.-
22/F-396 plying on Kollapur to Ramdurga road which was driven 
by the respondent herein had met with an accident when boy 
riding the cycle was trying to cross the road from right side to 
left side of the bus had dashed against the bumper of the bus 
sustaining major injuries.  
7. 
From this report, it is clear that the boy riding the cycle 
suddenly came before the bus and dashed against the bumper. 
Therefore, at a initial stage, the petitioner-Corporation had come 
to a conclusion that the cyclist was responsible for the accident 
and the driver was not at fault. Subsequently, inquiry has been 
held and the respondent/driver is declared to be guilty of 
causing the accident and the punishment has been imposed on 
him reducing three increments permanently. The tribunal having 
considered the evidence adduced by the parties and also the 
documents which were produced and marked by the parties has 
come to the conclusion that the respondent/driver was not at 


--- PAGE 5 ---
 
 
 
5
fault in committing the accident. The tribunal has observed that 
the reporter who was examined before the inquiring officer had 
not stated that the respondent/driver was rash and negligent in 
driving the bus and he was responsible for causing the death 
and during the cross examination of the said reporter before the 
inquiring officer he has admitted that he has submitted a report 
stating that the respondent/driver was not at fault.  The tribunal 
has also taken note of the fact that the respondent/driver was 
acquitted by the jurisdiction Magistrate in C.C.No.107/1995 
which was registered against him for driving the bus in a rash 
and negligent manner and causing the death of the cyclist. The 
tribunal having observed that since there was no material to 
show that the respondent/driver was driving the bus in a rash 
and negligent manner on the day at the time when the alleged 
accident had taken place on 6/9/1995, has rightly set aside the 
order of punishment imposed on the respondent/driver by 
petitioner-Corporation. Hence, I find no good grounds to 
interfere with the said order. Accordingly, the writ petition is 
dismissed.
