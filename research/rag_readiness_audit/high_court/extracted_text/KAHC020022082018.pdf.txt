ORDER 
Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner  
2. 
Petitioner 
is 
before 
this 
Court 
being 
aggrieved by the order passed by the Court of the II 
Additional Civil Judge and III Additional JMFC, Sirsi in 
O.S. No.334/2016 dated 03.04.2018, whereby the Trial 
Court has been pleased to reject the interlocutory 
application I.A. No.3 preferred by the plaintiff under 
Order 26 Rule 9 of CPC seeking appointment of a 
Commissioner for local inspection to ascertain the 
extent of encroachment. The Trial Court while rejecting 
the application has relied on the ruling of this Court in 
Miss Renuka V. Tammana and others reported in ILR 
2007 KAR 3029 and has placed reliance on the 
observations of this Court, as set out in paragraph 7 of 
the said judgment.  
3. 
There can be no quarrel with the proposition 
that has been set out by the Co-ordinate Bench of this 
Court. It is settled law that the appointment of 
Commissioner under the provision of Order 26 Rule 9 of 


--- PAGE 3 ---
 
 
- 3 - 
CPC cannot be an exercise in collecting evidence. 
Hence, the rejection by the Trial Court at the present 
stage cannot be faulted with. But it also remains a fact 
that by merely rejecting the application and not 
permitting the applicant to revive such an application at 
a later stage in the event of any ambiguity even after the 
Trial, in the opinion of this Court, is an error committed 
by the Trial Court.  
In that view of the matter, the order impugned 
stands modified. The rejection of the I.A. No.3 is 
affirmed. But at the same time, it is just and necessary 
to reserve liberty to the petitioner to revive the 
application in the event the petitioner so desires after 
the completion of recording of evidence.  
With the above observations, the petition stands 
disposed off.
