ORDER 
 
1. 
This petition is filed by the petitioners-plaintiffs in O.S. 
No.299 of 2008 on the file of the Principal Senior Civil Judge and 
JMFC, Anekal, is directed against the impugned order dated 
31.10.2023 passed on I.A. No.4 filed by respondent No.2—
defendant No.3 under Order VI rule 17 of the Civil Procedure Code, 
1908 (CPC) seeking amendment of the written statement, whereby 
the Trial Court allowed the said application filed by respondent 
No.2. 
2. 
The material on record discloses that the petitioners—
plaintiffs instituted the aforesaid suit against the respondents—
defendants for partition and separate possession of their alleged 
share in the suit schedule immoveable properties.   
3. 
The said suit is being contested the respondents-defendants. 
4. 
Prior to the commencement of the trail, respondent No.2—
defendant No.3 filed the instant application I.A. No.4 under Order 
VI Rule 17 of CPC seeking to incorporate certain subsequent 
events that were alleged to have transpired during the pendency of 
the suit.

5. 
The said application in I.A. No.4 having been opposed by the 
petitioners—plaintiffs, the Trial Court passed the impugned order 
allowing the said application.  Aggrieved by which, the petitioners 
are before this Court by way of the present writ petition. 
6. 
A perusal of the material on record will indicate that by way 
of the proposed amendment, respondent No.2—defendant No.3 
has sought to introduce certain subsequent events that are alleged 
to have transpire/occurred during the pendency of the suit, by 
incorporating paragraph 8(a) after paragraph 8 of their written 
statement. 
7. 
Though various contentions were urged by respondent 
No.2—defendant No.3 in the proposed amendment to the written 
statement, which are seriously disputed, denied and opposed by 
the petitioners—plaintiffs, in the light of the well-settled principles of 
law that the merits/demerits of the proposed amendments cannot 
be gone into while consider the application for amendment, as held 
by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in the case of RAJESH KUMAR 
AGGARWAL vs. K.K MODI, AIR 2006 SC 1647, I am of the 
considered opinion that the impugned order passed by the Trial 
Court is in conformity / consonance with the principles relating to 


--- PAGE 5 ---

the amendment of pleadings, as enunciated by the Apex Court in 
the case of LIFE INSURANCE CORPORATION OF INDIA vs. 
SANJEEV BUILDERS PRIVATE LIMITED & ANOTHER, AIR 
2022 SC 4256. 
8. 
At any rate, no prejudice can be said to have been caused to 
the petitioners—plaintiffs, who would be entitled to file their 
rejoinder / reply to the amended written statement. 
9. 
In these circumstances, it is clear that the impugned order 
passed by the Trial Court allowing I.A. No.4 cannot be said to have 
resulted in miscarriage of justice warranting interference by this 
Court in exercise of its jurisdiction under Article 227 of the 
Constitution of India, as held in RADHE SHYAM & ANOTHER VS. 
CHHABI NATH & OTHERS  - (2015) 5 SCC 423. 
10. 
In the result, I pass the following order: 
ORDER 
(i) 
The writ petition is hereby disposed of 
without interfering with the impugned 
order.
