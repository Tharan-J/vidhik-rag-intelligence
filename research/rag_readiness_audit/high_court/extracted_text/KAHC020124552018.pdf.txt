29. 
On a reading of clause 2 it is apparent that the 
parties agreed to conclude the transaction within one year 
from the date of agreement for sale.  It is also noticed that 
the obligation to execute the sale deed within one year 
was on the defendants after receipt of the balance 
consideration amount of  ₹11,00,000/- out of the total 
consideration of ₹12,25,000/-.  Thus the plaintiff had the 
obligation to pay the balance amount within one year.   
30. 
The said clause No.3 would also indicate that in 
case the sale deed is not executed within one year either 
to the plaintiff or anybody nominated by the plaintiff, the 
defendants are liable to pay interest at the rate of 16% 
per annum.  The default clause and the consequence of 
default clause incorporated in the agreement for sale 
would indicate that the plaintiff was not keen on the

the agreement would indicate that the parties had also

the time was the essence of the contract.  and damages 
for to breach in also agreed upon and plaintiff had agreed 
to receive compensation in case of default on the part of 
the defendants execute the sale deed even after receipt of 
sale consideration amount. 
31. 
It is further relevant to note that the plaintiff 
has not produced any records to show that he issued

has been repeatedly requesting the defendants to execute 
the sale deed, the plaintiff has not produced materials to 
show that he was ready with the balance consideration of 
₹11,00,000/- which is a substantial part of the sale 
consideration amount agreed.

32. 
This being the position, this court is of the view 
that the plaintiff did not exercise his right to seek specific 
performance by insisting the defendants to execute the 
sale deed by offering the balance amount of ₹11,00,000/- 
within one year.  There is nothing on record to indicate 
that ₹11,00,000/- was ready and available with the 
plaintiff before expiry of a 12 months from the date of the 
agreement for sale.  Under these circumstances, this court 
is of the view that the plaintiff is not entitled to the relief 
of specific performance of contract and is entitled to relief 
of refund of the earnest consideration amount.
