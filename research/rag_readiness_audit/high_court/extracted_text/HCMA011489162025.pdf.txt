ORDER
This writ petition has been filed seeking issuance of a writ of 
mandamus directing the respondents 1 and 2 to take action against the third 
respondent, which is allegedly permitting gambling activities inside the club.
2. Heard Mr.K.Raja, learned counsel for petitioner and Mr.Stalin 
Abhimanyu, learned Additional Government Pleader appearing for 
respondents.
3. The specific case of the petitioner is that he is a member of the 
club and that the third respondent club was using the club for gambling 
activities. Hence, a police raid was conducted on 18.06.2025 in the premises 
belonging to the third respondent. Thereafter, a representation was given to 
the respondents 1 and 2 to initiate action against the club and to stop the 
gambling activities inside the club. Since the same was not considered, the

act upon the representation made by the petitioner and conduct enquiry and 
take necessary action if gambling activities are going on in the club, within a 
period of two (2) weeks from the date of receipt of a copy of this order.
This writ petition is disposed of with the above direction. No 
costs. Consequently, connected miscellaneous petition is closed.
