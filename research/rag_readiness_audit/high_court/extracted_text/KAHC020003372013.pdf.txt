ORDER 
The grievance of the petitioners essentially relates 
to the quantum of benefits derivable by them under the 
Scheme in question evolved by the management on the 
closure of the industry. There is some dispute as to what is 
meant by the “last drawn wages” for the purpose of 
computing terminal benefits under the said Scheme. 
2.  
The 
respondent 
on 
service 
of 
notice 
entered appearance through the learned CGSC who opposes 
the writ petitions stating that if at all the petitioner had any 
grievance, they should try to explore the availability of 
redressal at the management level itself, and this having not 
been done, no relief could be granted to them at the hands 
of this Court. There is some force in this submission. 
However, he fairly submits that, if a comprehensive 
representation is made, there would not be much difficulty in

In the above circumstances, these writ

forms for seeking redressal, the Respondent shall make the 
same available so that the petitioners shall seek redressal for 
their grievance, accordingly.

consideration, since the main matter is disposed off.
