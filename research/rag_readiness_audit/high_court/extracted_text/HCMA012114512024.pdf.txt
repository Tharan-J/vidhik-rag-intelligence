O R D E R
By consent of both the learned counsel appearing for the petitioners as 
well as respondents, this writ petition is disposed of at the admission stage 
itself.
2. The petitioners herein seek a direction to the respondents to 
consider their representation dated 04.11.2024 seeking lease of fishing 
rights in the Jerthalav Eri, Palacode, Dharmapuri District in favour of 3rd 
respondent-Society for a period from 2024-2025 to 2028-2029 by applying 
Government Order in G.O.M.S.No.332, Animal Husbandry and Fisheries

3. It is the case of the petitioners that they are all Members of the 3rd 
respondent-Co-operative Society. It is stated that all the Members of the 3rd 
respondent-Society are Inland Fishermen and they are depending on fishing 
activities carried in and around the lakes situated in Dharmapuri District. It 
is also stated that the petitioners are poor fishermen and there is no other 
income to lead their livelihood. The Palacode Jerthalav Eri is under the 
control of the 1st respondent. Previously, the fishery rights in the above 
mentioned lake leased out in favour of the 3rd respondent-Society for 5 years 
from 2017-18 to 2021-22. The above said lease was given in favour of the 
3rd respondent-Society by following the guideline issued regarding 
preferential rights under G.O.M.S.No.332, Animal Husbandry and Fisheries 
(F.S.IV) Department, dated 17.11.1993. Now, the lease period of 5 years is 
already over in October, 2022.
4. It is also stated that except 3rd respondent-Society, there is no other 
fishermen society in the locality. Therefore, it is the case of the petitioners 
that by applying the guideline issued under G.O.M.S.No.332, Animal
