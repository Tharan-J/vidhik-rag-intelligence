COMMON ORDER
The learned Additional Advocate General probably has directed the 
Officials to come with the working sheet in terms of the statutory provisions. 
Accordingly, a working sheet is now produced before this Court by the 
respondents showing balance amounts payable by the respondents/contemners to 
the petitioners in terms of decree of Civil Court.  However, still there is a small 
discrepancy that was noticed by this Court.  Since the respondents have come 
forward with the statement that was prepared adding interest upto 30.04.2021, this 
Court is of the view that the difference may be due to some error in interest 
component, and that Contempt Petitions can be closed.  The petitioner's counsel 
may also verify the working sheet that has now been filed by the respondents in 
each case and note.  Since the respondents have now come with the calculation, 
which is in tune with the provisions of the Land Acquisition Act, and taking into 
consideration the decree that was granted by the Civil Court, this Court has to 
consider as to whether the delay is bona-fide and in good faith or not.  The learned 
Additional Advocate General made submissions to convince this Court, that the 
previous working sheet was due to bonafide mistake.  This Court is of the view 
that the delay in payment may be due to some bona-fide mistake in calculating the

amount as per the statutory provisions and the decree.  Since the respondent has 
agreed with the quantum as claimed by the petitioner in each case and undertaken 
to make payment without delay, this Court is inclined to close the contempt

the Contempt Petitions are closed based on the undertaking that the respondents 
will make the payment as per the calculation/working sheet that is now produced 
by the learned Additional Advocate General before this Court, accepting the 
liability and to pay the amounts on or before 31.05.2021.  However, the interest 
upto 31.05.2021 shall be calculated till the actual date of payment.
3.
As pointed out by this Court about the discrepancies regarding 
quantum, the petitioner's counsel should also take up the matter to the Government 
by pointing out mistake or error in the working sheet now produced by the 
respondents before this Court or the execution Court, so that the small difference 
can also be sorted out.
4.
As pointed out by this Court, the small discrepancy may be due to 
some error by inadvertence.  This can be sorted out by the petitioners in the 
pending E.P. proceedings.  Hence, all the Contempt Petitions are closed, subject to 
the liberty to the petitioners to get the actual amount payable by the respondents as
