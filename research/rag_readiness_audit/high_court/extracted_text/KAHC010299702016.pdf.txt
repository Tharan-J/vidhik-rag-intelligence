JUDGMENT 
 
 
This appeal by the claimants seeking enhancement of 
compensation is filed challenging the judgment and award dated  
August 04, 2015 in MVC No.3846/2014 passed by the Judge, 
Court of Small Causes & XXVI ACMM, Bengaluru.  
 
2. 
For the sake of convenience, parties shall be referred as 
per their status before the Tribunal. 
 
3. 
Heard Shri Vasanthappa, learned advocate for claimants-
appellants and Shri A.M.Venkatesh, learned advocate for second 
respondent-insurer. 


--- PAGE 3 ---
 
 
 
 
3
4. 
Claimants are parents of one Shri Manjunath who 
sustained grievous injuries in a road traffic accident on 
11.08.2014 when a Ambulance Van bearing Registration  
No.AP-02-X-3465 which he was driving dashed against a Lorry 
bearing Registration No.AP-03-X-9526 parked on the road 
without any signal or indication.  Shri Manjunath  sustained 
grievous injuries and died on the spot.   On adjudication of the 
claim petition, Tribunal has awarded Rs.12,20,000/-. 
 
5. 
Learned advocate for the claimants submitted that 
deceased was working as a driver of Ambulance Van and also 
doing agricultural work.  He was earning Rs.22,000/- per month.  
Learned Tribunal has erred in considering the income of the 
deceased as Rs.7,500/- p.m. and sought for enhancement. 
 
6. 
Learned Advocate for the insurer argued opposing the 
appeal. 
 
7. 
We have carefully considered rival submissions and 
perused the records. 
 


--- PAGE 4 ---
 
 
 
 
4
8. 
Claimants have produced salary certificate and RTC of the 
agricultural land owned by the deceased  as per Exhibit P8 and 
Exhibit P9, respectively.    The Tribunal has held that there is no 
conclusive document to show that deceased was getting salary 
of Rs.22,000/- p.m. and considered the notional income at 
Rs.7,500/- p.m.  Claimants have produced Ex.P8, salary 
certificate of deceased and one Paramesh examined as PW.3 has 
stated that he was paying salary of Rs.12,000/-.  There is no 
other material such as employee's register, salary paid receipts 
etc., to corroborate the payment of salary.  Therefore, in our 
view, Tribunal has rightly held that income of deceased has not 
been proved and considered notional income. This Court has 
consistently considered the notional income of an able bodied 
person in the year 2014 as Rs.8,500/- p.m. 
 
9. 
Deceased was aged 26 years, hence the applicable 
multiplier is 17 and future prospects is to be added at 40%.   
 
10. 
Claimants are parents of the deceased.  Since deceased 
was a Bachelor, 50% of his income is deductible while calculating 
loss of dependency.  Claimants are entitled to Rs.40,000/- each

13.  The Tribunal has held that there is contributory negligence  
to the extent of 75% on the part of the driver of the Lorry and 
25% on the part of deceased himself.  This finding is not 
challenged nor any arguments advanced in that behalf.  Hence, 
the claimants are entitled for 75% of total compensation which 
works out to Rs.9,92,850/- (Rs.13,23,800*75%). 
14. 
Hence, the following; 
ORDER 
(i)  
Appeal is allowed in part by holding that 
respondents No.1 & 2 are jointly and severally liable to 
pay

till the date of deposit.  The enhanced compensation is 
Rs.77,850/-;   and 
 
(ii)
