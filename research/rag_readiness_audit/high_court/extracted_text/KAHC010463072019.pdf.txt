THIS APPEAL COMING ON FOR THIS DAY, CHIEF 
JUSTICE DELIVERED THE FOLLOWING: 
 
JUDGMENT 
On instructions, the learned counsel appearing for the 
appellant states that the bank guarantee has been encashed 
by the respondent.  The prayer made by the appellant in a 
petition under Section 9 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 
1996 was for injunction restraining invocation of the bank 
guarantee.  By the order impugned, ad interim injunction has 
been refused by the learned Single Judge.  Now that the bank 
guarantee has been invoked and paid, the appellant will have 
to seek relief in appropriate proceedings in accordance with 
law.
