ORDER
This Writ Petition has been filed challenging the order
dated 14.03.2018 passed by the respondent rejecting the
petitioner's application for grant of electricity service
connection to his house bearing Door No.1023-A, situated in Survey
No.65/11 in Kattukaruppankottam Village, Aathanur Majira,
Thuraiyur Taluk, Trichirappalli District.
2. According to the petitioner, the impugned order dated
14.03.2018 passed by the respondent is by total non application of
mind and illegal.  It is his case that the demand made by the
petitioner towards compensation from him for the alleged theft of
electricity committed by him to the tune of Rs.6,44,835/- is the

6. The respondent has rejected the application of the
petitioner for grant of electricity service connection for his
house only on the ground that the petitioner has not paid a sum of
Rs.6,44,835/- as detailed above towards compensation for alleged
theft of electricity committed by him.  In the impugned order,
they have directed the petitioner to withdraw the Writ Petitions
filed by him challenging the demand made by the respondent towards

compensation for the alleged theft of electricity committed by
him.  When the petitioner has challenged the demand made by the
respondent towards compensation allegedly payable by the
petitioner towards theft of electricity, the respondent cannot
reject the petitioner's application for grant of electricity
service connection on the ground that the petitioner has not paid
the compensation unless and until the petitioner withdraws the
writ petitions filed by him challenging the demand for
compensation towards theft of electricity. 
7. It is a categorical stand of the petitioner as seen
from the affidavit filed in support of this Writ Petition as well
as the affidavit filed in support of the Writ Petitions namely
W.P.(MD).Nos.17480 of 2014, 10695 and 10696 of 2018, he has not
committed any theft of electricity.  When the demand for a sum of
Rs.6,44,835/- payable towards compensation for the alleged theft
of electricity by the petitioner is the subject matter of
challenge in  W.P.(MD).Nos.17480 of 2014, 10695 and 10696 of 2018,
by total non application of mind, the respondent issued the
impugned order dated 14.03.2018, rejecting the petitioner's
application for grant of electricity service connection on the
ground that the petitioner has not paid total sum of Rs.6,44,835/-
in respect of two existing connections as compensation towards
alleged theft of electricity committed by the petitioner.
 8. The learned counsel appearing for the petitioner has
rightly contended that the impugned order has been passed by the
respondent is arbitrary and has been passed by total non
application of mind.  The petitioner has also complied with the
conditional order passed by this Court in W.P.(MD).Nos.17480 of
2014, 10695 and 10696 of 2018 and therefore, the stay of the
earlier impugned order calling upon the petitioner to pay the
compensation towards alleged theft of electricity, continues.
When the demand for compensation towards alleged theft of
electricity has been stayed by this Court, the respondent has
passed the impugned order dated 14.03.2018 rejecting the
petitioner's application for grant of electricity service
connection on the ground that the petitioner has failed to pay the
compensation amount of Rs.6,44,835/- towards theft of electricity
and has not withdrawn the earlier writ petitions. 
9. The reasons given  by the respondent for rejection of
the petitioner's application for grant of electricity service
connection is arbitrary, illegal and has been passed by total non
application of mind to the interim orders granted by this Court in
W.P.(MD).Nos.17480 of 2014, 10695 and 10696 of 2018, which have
been filed by the petitioner challenging the very same demand
i.e., a sum of Rs.6,44,835/-mentioned in the impugned order dated
14.03.2018.

10. However, the petitioner will have to necessarily
satisfy all other statutory requirements for grant of electricity
service connection.  It is for the respondent to consider the
petitioner's application on merits and in accordance with law and
thereafter,  decide as to whether the petitioner is entitled for
grant of electricity service connection afresh or not for his
property.  However, it is made clear that the petitioner need not
pay the compensation charges for the alleged theft of electricity
amounting to Rs.6,44,835/- till the disposal of the Writ Petitions
in W.P.(MD).Nos.17480 of 2014, 10695 and 10696 of 2018
11. The petitioner is directed to submit a fresh
application to the respondent for grant of electricity service
connection to his property at Door No.1023-A, situated in Survey
No.65/11 in Kattukaruppankottam Village, Aathanur Majira,
Thuraiyur Taluk, Trichirappalli District, within a period of one
week from the date of receipt of a copy of this order and on
receipt of the same, the respondent shall pass final orders on
merits and in accordance with law, within a period of four weeks
thereafter without demanding the sum of Rs.6,44,835/- claimed
towards theft of electricity which is the subject matter in W.P.
(MD).Nos.17480 of 2014, 10695 and 10696 of 2018.
12. The Writ Petition stands disposed of accordingly.  No
costs.
