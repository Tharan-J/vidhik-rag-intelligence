THIS CRP IS FILED UNDER SECTION 115 OF CPC, 
PRAYING  TO SET ASIDE THE ORDER ON IA NO.7 IN OS 
NO.163/2015 ON THE FILE OF CIVIL JUDGE AND JUDICIAL 
MAGISTRATE FIRST CLASS, HADAGALI DATED 07.02.2022 IN 
THE ENDS OF JUSTICE AND ETC.  
 
THIS REVISION PETITION COMING ON FOR ORDERS 
THIS DAY, THE COURT MADE THE FOLLOWING: 
 
ORDER 
 
1. 
The petitioner is before this Court seeking for the following  
reliefs: 
a. 
This Hon’ble Court be pleased to set aside the 
order on I.A.No.7 in O.S.No.163/2015 on the file 
of Civil Judge and JMFC, Hadagali dated 
07.02.2022 in the ends of justice. 
b. 
Consequently the suit of the plaintiff in OS 
No.163/2015 be dismissed by allowing the IA 
No.7 in the ends of justice. 
 
2. 
O.S.No.163/2015 has been filed seeking for the following 
reliefs: 
a. 
Declaring that the plaintiffs are absolute owners 
of the Schedule ‘A’ property; 
b. 
Directing the defendant to deliver the possession 
of the Schedule ‘B’ property to the plaintiffs; 
c. 
Grant 
Mandatory 
injunction 
directing 
the 
defendant to demolish the construction of 
compound wall lying in the Schedule ‘B’ 
property. 
d. 
Grant such other reliefs as deemed fit by this 
Hon’ble Court under the facts and circumstances 
of the case and award the costs of the suit in the 
interest of justice and equity.

3. 
In the said suit, an application under Order VII Rule 11 (a) 
and (d) of the Code of Civil Procedure was filed seeking for 
rejection of the plaint on the ground that the plaint is 
hopelessly time barred and the same does not disclose a 
valid cause of action. 
4. 
The trial Court having considered the matter dismissed the 
said application on the ground that the application is belated.  
The said application was filed only to protract the 
proceedings when the matter was posted for arguments and 
the grounds said to have been raised as regards the 
limitation are subject to the trial already having been 
conducted and the same would be considered during the 
course of the final arguments.    It is aggrieved by the same, 
the petitioner is before this Court challenging the said order. 
5. 
Sri. Shrikant Patil, learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that the cause of action which has been pleaded in the plaint 
at para 13 is make believe the allegation which have been 
made are only made so as to suit the requirement of the 
plaintiff.  The suit is barred by limitation inasmuch as the 
property having been purchased in the year 1976-77, the suit

having been filed in the year 2015, and as such, the plaint is

6. 
He relies upon the decision of the Hon’ble Apex Court in the 
case of Dahiban Vs. Arvindbhai Kalyanji Bhanusali (D) 
and Ors. Reported in (2020) 7 SCC 366, more particularly 
para 12.9, to contend that the power under Order VII Rule 11 
of the Code of Civil Procedure may be exercised  at any 
stage of the suit.  Filing of the application at the stage of 
arguments would not disentitle the consideration of the 
application filed by the petitioner. 
7. 
In view of the order proposed to be made, notice to 
respondents is dispensed with. 
8. 
Admittedly, the suit is one for declaration of title along with 
delivery of possession and for mandatory injunction directing 
the defendant to demolish the construction of compound 
wall.  The plaintiff in the plaint has stated that the property 
had been purchased in the year 1976-77 and the RTC was 
mutated in the name of Smt.Basamma and Thotaiah, who 
were stated to be in occupation.  Thereafter, the husband of

subsequently, the name of plaintiff No.1, who is son of 
Basamma, was mutated in the RTC.  Subsequently he has 
taken up the matter and issued notice to the respondents.  
On receipt of the reply, the suit was filed. 
9. 
In view of the said statements made in the plaint, it cannot be 
ex-facie said that the suit is barred by limitation.  The 
question of limitation is a mixed question of law and fact, 
which requires trial.  Whether in fact the possession of the 
property was with the defendant or not and the date on which 
defendant came into possession of the property or whether 
the allegation of the plaintiff that the plaintiff is the owner of 
the property and entitled for possession is a matter which is 
required to be decided after trial.   
10. 
In the present matter, the trial Court has already taken place 
and the matter is now posted for arguments.  Though the 
application under Order VII Rule 11 of the Code of Civil 
Procedure can be filed at any stage, I am of the considered 
opinion that, in the present matter, when the aspects which 
have to be decided in respect of Order VII Rule 11 (a) & (d)
